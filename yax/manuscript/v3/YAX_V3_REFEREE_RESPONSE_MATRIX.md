## Bottom line

V3 no longer claims that the pre-outcome continuous support diagnostic is the exact influence map of the headline estimator. It reports continuous residual-treatment support and fitted headline conditional-information support as separate objects. It preserves the strongest confirmatory result—the negative sign across all pre-specified exposure architectures—while narrowing magnitude, causal, survey-uncertainty, and employment-stock interpretations.
