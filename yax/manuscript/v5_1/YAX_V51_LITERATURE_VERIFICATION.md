The V5.1 contribution statement is comparative, not priority-maximal: it does not claim the first multi-measure labor study, mapping repair, CPS young-worker analysis, or exposure-instability finding. It connects architecture, harmonization, effective support, estimator information, same-design stock estimates, and realized-movement classification.
