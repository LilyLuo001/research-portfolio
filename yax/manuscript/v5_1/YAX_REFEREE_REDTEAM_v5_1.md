# YAX V5.1 final referee red team

No perspective below recommends a new empirical extension; the permitted next work is editorial.

## Skeptical *Review of Economics and Statistics* editor

- **Strongest contribution:** a unusually complete treatment-measurement chain—native architecture, taxonomy, support, estimator information, same-design stock inference, and marginal ranking consequences—in a salient national labor application.
- **Strongest rejection reason:** the headline employment pattern is observational and the direct paired beta–alpha difference is imprecise; an editor may view the measurement chain as a sophisticated audit rather than a sufficiently broad economics result.
- **Remaining overstatement risk:** “robust aggregate direction” must always be tied to point estimates or the specified tail contrast, because one common-support interval includes zero and the simultaneous all-six claim fails.
- **Readiness:** technically ready for a one-shot submission after citation/version verification and journal formatting; acceptance risk remains high on contribution threshold rather than execution quality.

## Skeptical *Research Policy* editor

- **Strongest contribution:** exposure architectures embody different theories of technological capability and organizational complementarity, and the joint F+G result shows that family choice contains outcome-relevant information beyond a transparent consensus.
- **Strongest rejection reason:** the paper does not observe adoption, organizational change, or innovation strategy; a management-of-technology audience may want the link from potential exposure to firm behavior.
- **Remaining overstatement risk:** the positive `G` coefficient cannot be narrated as AIOE being “better,” more causal, or more complementary. It is a conditional between-family association.
- **Readiness:** ready if the introduction foregrounds technology measurement and administrative-data complementarity; do not add an adoption branch.

## Skeptical *Labour Economics* referee

- **Strongest contribution:** the CPS design independently documents the early-career stock tail direction and shows exactly how occupation measurement affects support and mobility interpretation.
- **Strongest rejection reason:** the stock coefficient does not identify hiring, exits, or displacement; the predeclared flow exercise is too imprecise to supply the missing mechanism.
- **Remaining overstatement risk:** the 53.28% conflict rate counts tiny sign changes, so it must remain a classification statistic and be paired with κ, magnitude caveat, and the hard-benchmark withdrawal.
- **Readiness:** ready after a final prose and citation pass. The unresolved flow mechanism is a limitation to disclose, not a reason for another model.

## Final assessment

V5.1 is internally coherent and submission-ready conditional on editorial checks. Its best pitch is not causal AI displacement. It is a general lesson for constructed treatments demonstrated in an economically important case: the same aggregate tail direction can coexist with non-interchangeable rankings, magnitude variation, and an outcome-relevant between-family dimension.
