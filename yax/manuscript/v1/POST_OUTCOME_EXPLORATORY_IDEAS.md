# POST-OUTCOME EXPLORATORY — NOT PART OF CONFIRMATORY YAX v1.1

## Joint pre-trend test

The confirmatory archive reports 65 non-reference pre-event coefficients with pointwise confidence intervals and a separate 2017–2019 placebo. It does not contain a joint test that all pre-event coefficients equal zero.

A joint pre-trend test could be considered only as explicitly post-outcome supplementary analysis if later authorized. It was not computed during manuscript revision round 2 and must not be represented as confirmatory evidence.
