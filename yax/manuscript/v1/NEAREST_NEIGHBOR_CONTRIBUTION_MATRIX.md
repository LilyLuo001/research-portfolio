## Narrow contribution statement

> Existing work shows that occupational AI-exposure measures can be unstable across annotators, platforms, score construction, and mapping choices. YAX contributes an integrated frozen chain from cross-family measurement architecture to construct diagnostics, effective identifying occupations, mapping and common support, and same-design labor inference. Its substantive finding is not universal coefficient divergence: the architectures and identifying comparisons diverge markedly, while the post-ChatGPT young-relative employment-stock gradient remains negative across every frozen exposure definition and its cross-measure magnitude remains imprecisely distinguished.
