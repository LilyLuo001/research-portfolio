# Owner sign-off: Test C SESOI rule

**Recorded:** 2026-08-28, before any protected post-period outcome was opened.

The owner approves the following binding rule for Test C:

    SESOI = 25% × |final literature-comparable Q5–Q1 benchmark|

This record approves a **rule**, not a numerical SESOI. The numerical value is
determined mechanically only after the literature benchmark has been placed on
exactly the same age band, outcome, Q5–Q1 exposure contrast, unit/estimand and
functional scale as Test C. A headline magnitude on another estimand must not be
substituted.

## Economic interpretation

A change in the literature-comparable employment contrast equal to at least one
quarter of the contested benchmark, caused solely by changing the definition of
AI exposure, is an economically meaningful measurement consequence.

The 25% fraction is chosen for economic meaning, not statistical convenience.
It will not change after equivalence power is examined. If the outcome-blind
paired-equivalence analysis is underpowered at this bound, the paper will report
that the design cannot establish equivalence. The bound will not be widened.

The 12.5%, 25% and 50% benchmark fractions are reported as design diagnostics;
25% remains primary regardless of their power.

## Seal

This sign-off reads no protected post-period outcome and changes no outcome,
age group, exposure measure, estimator, fixed effect, mechanism or inference
rule. It resolves only plan v5 §4.3 and seal-protocol item 5c.
