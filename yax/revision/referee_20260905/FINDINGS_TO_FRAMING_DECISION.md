# Findings-to-framing decision

All results in this document are independently reproduced or newly estimated in the revision branch. New analyses are post-outcome exploratory and do not alter YAX v1.1 confirmatory status.

| Question | Verified evidence | Framing implication |
|---|---|---|
| Is the stock contrast distinctive to AI exposure? | On identical 363-occupation support, beta is -0.1142. Wage, education, cognitive content, telework, and STEM benchmarks are -0.0185, +0.0093, +0.0270, -0.0240, and -0.0161. Paired beta-minus-benchmark intervals exclude zero for education, cognition, and telework, but not wage or STEM. A within-SOC2 permutation diagnostic places the observed coefficient at lower-tail probability .219 (centered two-sided .434). | The observed pattern is more negative than several selected occupational characteristics, but a broad-family permutation does not establish AI specificity. The paper must say **AI specificity is not established**. |
| Is the Q5--Q1 estimate a high-tail effect? | Q5--Q1 is -0.1311; Q5--Q2 is -0.0456 and Q5--Q4 is -0.0340, with both intervals including zero. Q5--Q3 is -0.0833 with an interval excluding zero. Fast-food deletion attenuates the estimate; Q1/Q5 paths show distinct movements in both tails. | The low-exposure reference matters. The headline must describe a tail contrast and cannot be summarized as high-exposure deterioration alone. |
| Does the comparison-age pool create the result? | 22--25 versus 26--35 is -0.1307, virtually identical to 22--25 versus 26--65 (-0.1311; paired difference +0.0003). The contrast is smaller against ages 51--65 (-0.0978; paired difference +0.0333, interval excluding zero). The six-bin age profile estimates +0.0645 for ages 31--40 relative to 51--65. | Pooling ages 26--65 does not explain the result relative to the nearest comparison group, but the chosen older reference matters. The age estimand belongs in the headline description. |
| Does the post-2025 extension change the conclusion? | The coefficient is -0.1356 in 2023, -0.0783 in 2024, and -0.1629 in 2025--26. The last differs from 2024 by -0.0846, but not from 2023. | The era average is not monotone. The extension renews the contrast relative to 2024; it does not create an unprecedented break relative to 2023. |
| Is the F/G asymmetry an alpha artifact? | Omitting alpha leaves F=-0.0330 and G=+0.0342. G intervals cross zero when beta or broad is omitted but not when an AIOE measure is omitted. Direct D and S coefficients are both negative. | The original G sign is not alpha-only, but its precision is construction-sensitive within the Eloundou family. Withdraw any family-general ranking. |
| Are exposure rankings economically non-interchangeable? | Zero-threshold six-way directional conflict is 53.28%; movement-mass weighting lowers it to 40.10%; substantial opposition falls to 29.79% at 0.25 SD and 14.00% at 0.5 SD. Aggregate moving-higher shares remain 49.2--50.5%. The hard no-self rematching benchmark leaves a 0.96-point excess. | Architecture choice changes labels for many small or moderate moves, but aggregate direction is stable. The mobility section should emphasize scale-sensitive ranking fragility, not broad aggregate reallocation disagreement. |
| Does taxonomy repair matter? | Computer/mathematical employment coverage rises from 3.33% under exact codes to 97.7% after repair. The continuous coefficient changes -0.01885 to -0.01920 on fixed support, then to -0.03156 when support expands; excluding computer/math yields -0.02940. | Repair primarily changes who enters the estimand, not scores among previously matched occupations. This is a substantive measurement result, while the unrepaired merge is not a preferred alternative. |
| Do outside architectures corroborate the six-measure result? | Webb AI is -0.0649 and OECD capability exposure is -0.0110; both intervals include zero. On common supports, differences from beta are +0.0646 and +0.1115, respectively, with the OECD difference barely spanning zero. | The selected six implementations do not span the architecture space. The paper must distinguish within-family sensitivity from between-concept comparison and center the contribution on constructed-treatment uncertainty. |
| Framing decision | Evidence supports a documented measurement application, not a causal employment-effect paper and not an all-architectures robustness claim. | **Adopt title:** “Constructed Exposure Measures and Statement-Specific Robustness: Evidence from Early-Career Employment.” Lead with construction, taxonomy, reference-tail, architecture-set, and ranking results; retain the -0.1311 estimate as the application baseline, not the organizing discovery. |
