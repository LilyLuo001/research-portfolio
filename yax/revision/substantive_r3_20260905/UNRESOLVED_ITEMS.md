# Substantive R3 unresolved items

This memo distinguishes genuine evidence limits from unfinished execution.  The
revision package has completed every feasible registered analysis.  The items
below cannot be repaired by another unrestricted specification search on the
authorized data.

1. **Employer adoption and employer flows.** CPS has no employer identifier,
   observed AI-adoption measure, or employer-match hiring and separation
   margins.  The public-CPS bridge to the proprietary BCC analysis is therefore
   approximate and cannot be promoted to a replication.
2. **Exact BCC group membership.** The inspected public materials do not reveal
   a complete occupation membership, cutoff, and tie algorithm.  The revision
   reports two documented public approximations and labels both as such.
3. **Complete CPS design inference.** Public IPUMS Basic Monthly files do not
   provide every confidential stratum and PSU identifier needed for full
   design-based variance estimation.  Occupation shocks, 22-family shocks,
   household/sample-unit resampling, elapsed-time HAC, and the stress simulation
   answer different questions and are not mechanically added.
4. **Age-specific historical routing.** The pre-2020 occupation bridge provides
   common route shares, not validated age-specific shares.  Route conservation
   and bounded allocation sensitivities are reported; a missing validation
   sample is not fabricated.
5. **Construct-validation labels.** No external gold standard jointly measures
   occupation-task capability, realized time saving, adoption, and task
   importance.  Sampling error in CPS outcomes therefore does not cover
   exposure-measure error.
6. **Direct within-family precision.** Few broad families span both exposure
   tails.  Direct-tail, flow, subgroup, and several conditional estimates remain
   imprecise; a confidence interval containing zero is a nondetection, not
   evidence of economic equivalence.
7. **Causal timing.** The unrestricted joint preperiod tests reject in both
   dynamic structures.  January 2023 is consequently descriptive chronology,
   not an identified generative-AI intervention date.
8. **Failed coding-stable endpoint model.** The declared post-2020 static model
   did not converge and remains in the failure registry.  No alternative was
   selected after seeing that failure.
9. **Source-report version.** The two supplied referee reports are preserved
   verbatim.  The integrated execution prompt also cites a separately numbered
   source report that was not supplied; the response matrix maps its integrated
   requirements but does not claim to quote that missing source.
10. **Author-supplied metadata.** Affiliation, email, acknowledgments, funding,
    conflicts, and journal-specific disclosures remain `[AUTHOR TO COMPLETE]`.

These limits narrow the paper's contribution to a comparison-centered audit of
the public-CPS association.  They do not justify stronger causal, equivalence,
or proprietary-replication claims.
