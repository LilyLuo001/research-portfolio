## Result and its limit

The journal-by-journal, official-publisher-domain searches recorded in
`B09_TEN_JOURNAL_SEARCH_LEDGER.json` did not locate a peer-reviewed article that
combines all of the following: a national monthly CPS young-versus-older
employment-stock design, named occupational AI-exposure architectures, and
systematic variation in mapping or support construction while the outcome
design is held fixed.

This is a dated targeted search, not a systematic review. It does not prove
absence and does not establish priority for the broader young-worker or
public-CPS pattern. In particular, Emanuel, Harrington, and Pallais (QJE 2026)
already use CPS to study labor-market outcomes for young college graduates in
remotable occupations with AI-related controls. Brynjolfsson, Chandar, and Chen
(August 2026), outside the declared journal set, directly report the recent
young-worker pattern using ADP and public-data comparisons.

## Reproducible protocol

For each journal, the JSON ledger preserves:

- the exact domain-constrained query executed on 2026-09-08;
- the official publisher domain;
- the closest returned or independently located publisher record;
- verified authors, title, year or version, and publication status;
- a specific reason the candidate is related but not the exact YAX design.

Search results can change with indexing. The stable publisher URL, rather than
search-result rank or count, is the preserved evidence object. A rerun that
finds another close article should append it; it must not silently overwrite
this dated record.
