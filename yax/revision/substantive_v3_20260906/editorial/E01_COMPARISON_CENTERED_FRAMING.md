# E01 comparison-centered framing audit

Status: active-source revision complete; final rendered review remains.

The manuscript, online appendix, and referee response now use the title
"Occupational AI Exposure and Young-Worker Employment: Support and
Comparisons in the CPS." The retired question-form title suggested that the
design could decide between AI and occupational composition. It cannot: the
broad-family specifications compare conditional estimands and do not allocate
the observed movement causally.

The revised opening leads with the paper's actual target and the occupational
support carrying it. Its positive contribution is localization: the public-CPS
association is learned predominantly from comparisons spanning broad
occupation families, direct within-family resolution is limited, and taxonomy
and calendar choices can silently alter support. This is useful to later work
with observed adoption or quasi-experimental variation because it identifies
which broad-family comparisons such designs must replace rather than inherit.

`paper/scripts/audit_substantive_revision.py` fails if the retired title
returns to any active title-bearing source, if the comparison-centered title
is absent, or if the active paper loses the declared interpretation boundaries.
Completion still requires the unresolved empirical dependencies and visual
inspection of the compiled documents.
