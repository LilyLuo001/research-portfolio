## First-order decision gates

1. Does the detailed Q5--Q1 coefficient survive controls for broad-occupation-specific young-relative evolution?
2. How large a difference could the realized design detect for each architecture comparison, and are present comparative claims stronger than the paired intervals permit?
3. Does joint deletion or transparent down-weighting of influential occupations materially change the descriptive contrast?
4. Are the point estimate and uncertainty stable under quarterly aggregation and an inference procedure addressing CPS rotation-induced serial dependence?
5. Where does the January-2023 estimate lie in an identically constructed pseudo-break distribution from 2015--2019?

The answers to these gates, rather than the desired framing, determine the next manuscript center of gravity.
