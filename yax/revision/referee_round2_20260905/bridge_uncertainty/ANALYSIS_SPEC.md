# Taxonomy-bridge uncertainty analysis specification

Status: written after outcomes had been opened and before the analyses in this
directory were executed.  Every estimate produced here is **post-outcome
exploratory** and is not part of confirmatory YAX v1.1.  The frozen design and
its artifacts remain unchanged.

This bounded workstream responds to RR2-M5 and secondary comment 1.4.  It
separates three objects that the prior exposition combined: the corrected
survey calendar, the set of occupation clusters, and uncertainty introduced by
one-to-many Census-2010-to-Census-2018 routes.

## 1. Calendar and universe reconciliation

The substantive baseline is the corrected 113-month static calendar: January
2017 through July 2026, with March 2017--2021 restored, December 2022 omitted as
the declared transition month, and October 2025 absent because no CPS was
collected.  Its beta-by-Webb Q5--Q1 estimate must reproduce
`-0.1345539535732939`.  The frozen 108-month estimate `-0.13107397642233506`
is retained only as a chronology benchmark.

The reported 490 and 495 occupation counts will be reconstructed rather than
explained from labels:

- `490` is the balanced pre-period Census-2018 target support stored in the
  frozen 66-month pre-period cell artifact; the cell builder already requires
  full beta-exposure coverage as well as positive young and older stock.  It
  does not yet require Webb.
- `495` is the repaired-AIOE-plus-Webb finite-support subset of the
  route-expanded raw reconstruction over those same 66 months, after the same
  positive-stock rule.  The raw route-expanded candidate count is separately
  reported.  Thus 490 and 495 are architecture-specific subsets, not nested
  stages of one sample flow.  Their exact set relation will be reported.

The reconciliation will list every code in the union, its presence in each
universe, whether it can be generated by a split route, whether it appears
directly after 2020, and the availability of beta, repaired AIOE, and Webb.

## 2. Route-share diagnostics

Pre-2020 source occupations are classified using the official bridge as
one-to-one or one-to-many.  From 2020 onward raw `OCC` is already Census 2018
and is classified as direct.  Weighted stock will be reported separately for
ages 22--25 and 26--65, beta Q1, beta Q5, and the remaining quintiles, in:

1. the bridge-dependent 2017--2019 period;
2. the direct-taxonomy preperiod, 2020--November 2022; and
3. the postperiod, January 2023--July 2026.

For the corrected beta-by-Webb model, fitted conditional information for the
Q5 post coefficient will be computed after partialling the other included
slopes.  We will report (a) the information share in target occupations ever
touched by a split route and (b) a proportional cell attribution using the
split-stock share.  The latter is a diagnostic allocation, not an identified
information decomposition.

## 3. Clean-route sensitivity

The primary clean-route sensitivity restricts the corrected-calendar beta-by-
Webb model to target occupations with no structurally possible one-to-many
inbound route in the official bridge.  It holds the original support's beta
quintile labels and Webb normalization fixed, so coefficient movement is a
sample-support sensitivity rather than a redefinition of treatment.  A second,
separately labelled row recomputes quintiles and Webb normalization on the
clean support; it changes the comparison population and is not treated as the
same estimand.  The prior stable Census-2010 result is carried into the summary
with its source-file hash, not reinterpreted as an allocation correction.

## 4. Age-specific allocation uncertainty

Official bridge proportions are not age-specific.  The baseline therefore
applies the same proportions to young and older source records and mechanically
preserves each source occupation's age ratio across target components before
aggregation.  No alternative is called the correct allocation.

Two transparent diagnostics are predeclared:

1. **Accounting bounds.**  For each source-month-age cell and each fixed beta
   tail, allocate its stock anywhere among that source's officially allowed
   targets.  The lower bound assigns no stock to the tail unless every allowed
   target is in it; the upper bound assigns all stock to the tail whenever at
   least one allowed target is in it.  These are sharp stock-accounting bounds
   under unrestricted age-specific allocation across allowed routes; they are
   not coefficient bounds.
2. **Exposure-directed tilt scenarios.**  For split sources whose every target
   has finite beta and Webb scores and nonconstant beta, rank targets within
   source on beta and scale the rank to `[-0.5, 0.5]`.  Starting from official
   route weight `w_sj`, use
   `w_y,sj proportional to w_sj*exp(theta*r_sj)` and
   `w_o,sj proportional to w_sj*exp(-theta*r_sj)`.  The grid sets
   `K=exp(2*theta)` to `0.5, 2/3, 1, 1.5, 2`.  Thus `K` is the young-versus-
   older relative allocation odds at the highest- versus lowest-beta target.
   Ineligible split sources retain official weights.  Support, treatment
   labels, and Webb scaling remain fixed.  These are parameterized scenarios,
   not probability statements or identified bounds.

## 5. Outputs and refusal rules

Outputs contain aggregate cells and diagnostics only; no raw licensed CPS
records or credentials may enter the repository.  Every input and output is
hashed.  Self-checks require exact reproduction of both baseline coefficients,
mass conservation under every scenario, exact 490/495 counts or an explicit
failure, finite model results, and consistency of route-share sums.  If the
universe counts do not reconstruct, the script fails rather than backfilling an
explanation.  If a scenario cannot be implemented without assigning a missing
score, that source remains at official weights and its excluded stock share is
reported.
