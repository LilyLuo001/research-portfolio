# Dependence among the six initial exposure implementations

Status: post-outcome exploratory, treatment-side only. No labor outcome was read by this analysis.

The employment-weighted spectrum confirms that the six initial scores are highly dependent. On the exact frozen six-score-plus-Webb common support of 444 occupations, the first principal component explains 80.04 percent of score variance and the first two explain 96.11 percent. Two components are required to reach 95 percent; three explain 99.58 percent. The conclusion is unchanged on the 465-occupation six-score complete support and the 446-occupation six-score-plus-Webb model support.

This is a dependence diagnostic, not a latent-variable validation exercise. Principal components are not admitted as new exposure architectures. The result supports the referees' request to describe the original set as six implementations drawn from two tightly related construction families, rather than six independent validations of a single underlying quantity.

Exact eigenvalues, weighted correlations, supports, and input hashes are stored in the `results/` directory.
