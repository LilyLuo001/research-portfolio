# Findings-to-framing decision

**Revision status:** post-outcome, referee-led major revision; the frozen YAX
v1.1 record is preserved unchanged.

## Chosen center of gravity

The revised paper is a composition-centered measurement paper.  It asks
whether the early-career CPS association assigned to detailed occupational AI
exposure survives conditioning on broad occupational-family evolution, and
which measurement operations can and cannot be compared as if they were
alternative versions of one treatment.

This choice follows the new evidence rather than either referee's proposed
mechanism.  The corrected-calendar beta Q5--Q1 coefficient is -0.1346 log
points.  Adding SOC2-by-young-by-post controls reduces it to -0.0315; allowing
SOC2-specific monthly young-relative paths gives -0.0317.  The paired changes
are detected, but the conditioned estimates retain only about 30 percent of
baseline target information and change the conditioning estimand.  The paper
therefore describes the baseline as principally a broad-occupation comparison,
not as a causal decomposition and not as proof that detailed AI exposure is
irrelevant.

## Findings retained in the main paper

1. **Corrected substantive baseline.**  The March-restored 113-month estimate
   (-0.1346) replaces the known incomplete 108-month calendar as the default
   descriptive result.  The frozen estimate (-0.1311) and one canonical
   9,999-draw interval remain visible as the confirmatory record.
2. **Broad composition.**  SOC2-conditioned estimates, their paired movements,
   support loss, information loss, and the number of families spanning both
   tails are the central evidence.
3. **Reference and shape.**  The full quintile profile, a Q2--Q5 equality test,
   a monotonicity assessment, and the stable-tail estimate replace claims based
   on whether separate intervals cross zero.
4. **Influence without a manufactured mechanism.**  Joint leave-5/10/20,
   trimming, robust weighting, and clean service exclusions show meaningful
   influence but do not support the claim that food-service recovery explains
   the result.
5. **Resolution limits.**  The realized primary MDE is 0.1244 log points.  All
   seven paired beta-minus-alternative Q5--Q1 intervals contain zero, so the
   paper reports non-detection rather than architectural equivalence or
   heterogeneity.
6. **Direct literature bridge.**  BCC's reproducible public exposure grouping
   gives -0.0728 in CPS.  It is explicitly a grouping bridge, not a replication
   of proprietary ADP hiring or separation estimates.
7. **Measurement taxonomy.**  Implementation error, same-construct
   measurement, construct change, support/population change, estimand change,
   representation change, and coordinate reparameterization are separated.
   Exact invariance results and the architecture census make this operational.
8. **Mapping uncertainty.**  The 539/490/495 universes are reconciled; fixed-
   support repair is separated from population expansion; one-to-one and
   stable-taxonomy results and transparent age-allocation tilts bound what the
   available bridge can show.

## Findings demoted to the appendix

The F/G rotation is an exact coordinate change and is no longer substantive
evidence.  Mobility remains a diagnostic of constructed classifications.  Its
family-balanced disagreement, exact-support benchmark, sampling uncertainty,
repair-rule sensitivity, and entry-destination result do not identify hiring
or validate a misclassification correction.

## Claims explicitly retired

- that alternative significance patterns establish coefficient differences;
- that intervals containing zero establish economic equivalence;
- that six dependent implementations are six independent validations;
- that the detailed exposure gradient is identified as a causal AI effect;
- that broad-family conditioning is a causal decomposition;
- that food-service recovery explains the headline coefficient;
- that employed-to-employed switches reveal hiring;
- that immediate A--B--A reversals are a measured occupation-error rate;
- that taxonomy implementation errors are defensible alternative constructs;
- that a national adoption series, mechanically interacted with fixed exposure,
  would solve the identifying problem.

## Title decision

**AI Exposure or Occupational Composition? Constructed Measures and
Early-Career Employment**

The title foregrounds the empirical distinction the revision can actually
establish while retaining the broader measurement contribution.  It does not
claim a food-service mechanism or causal AI displacement.

This round resolves every numbered major comment in the two reports and the
