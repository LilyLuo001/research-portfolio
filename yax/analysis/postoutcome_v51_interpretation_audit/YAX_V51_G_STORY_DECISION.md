# YAX V5.1 G-story decision

## Decision: FRAME-G2

Keep the V5.1 statement-specific framing and treat G as bounded exploratory evidence.

The exact scaling audit is **AE-R1**. Reexpressing the already-estimated standardized F+G model in the original family-centroid coordinates implies an AIOE-family coefficient of +0.02496 per original unit (+0.02493 per weighted SD) and an Eloundou-family coefficient of -0.07094 per original unit (-0.06148 per weighted SD). The A-minus-E original-unit contrast is +0.09590, with covariance-transformed normal 95% interval [0.00645, 0.18535] and exact transformed existing-G wild-score interval [0.00559, 0.18621]. The linear predictor is preserved to `2.78e-17`.

The treatment-only construction audit is **G-PARTIAL**, not G-STABLE. Removing beta or broad leaves G highly stable. Removing alpha reduces the employment-weighted level/rank correlations to 0.865/0.855, retains 72.5% of Q1 and 69.5% of Q5 weight, and changes the sign around zero for 17.9% of employment. Thus the family-disagreement dimension partly reflects alpha's distinctive treatment geometry.

Because the combined gate requires both AE-R1 (or strong AE-R2) and G-STABLE, it does not pass. The title remains unchanged. The manuscript may explain the exact implied A/E asymmetry in Section 6.2, but it must immediately disclose G-PARTIAL and must not elevate the result into a general Eloundou-versus-AIOE economic story.

Allowed summary:

> The scaled reparameterization of the exploratory joint model places the negative conditional association on the Eloundou-family centroid rather than the AIOE-family centroid. Treatment-only leave-one-measure diagnostics show, however, that the family-disagreement coordinate changes materially when alpha is omitted. The result is therefore bounded evidence about this frozen construction, not a general ranking of exposure families.

Prohibited interpretations remain: “only Eloundou matters,” “AIOE has no effect,” “the Eloundou family is correct,” and any causal or adoption claim.

No new labor-outcome model was estimated. No leave-one-Eloundou outcome model and no leave-one-occupation-out outcome analysis was executed.
