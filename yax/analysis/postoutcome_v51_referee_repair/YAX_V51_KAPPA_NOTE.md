# YAX V5.1 kappa agreement note

The frozen realized-switch sample contains 108,500 adjacent-month occupational changes with finite origin and destination scores under all six architectures. Direction is coded `-1`, `0`, or `+1`; exact zero is the only tie. No deadband or alternative definition was searched.

All 15 pairwise Cohen κ statistics were computed twice: with the official origin `LNKFW1MWT` and unweighted. Official-weight raw exact agreement ranges from 55.37% to 96.58%; opposite-sign conflict ranges from 3.42% to 42.59%. Chance-adjusted κ ranges from 0.125 to 0.932. The lowest agreement is between direct-ability AIOE and Eloundou alpha; the highest is between administrative and source-employment-weighted AIOE. The corresponding unweighted ranges are nearly identical.

The standard unweighted six-rater Fleiss κ is 0.501 (observed pair agreement 0.747; chance agreement 0.494). A direct official-weighted descriptive analogue is 0.502 (observed 0.748; chance 0.494). The weighted value is labeled an analogue because ordinary Fleiss κ is defined for equally weighted items.

These results address base-rate sensitivity but do not replace the 53.28% any-opposite-sign statistic. Exposure architectures are constructed measures, not random human raters, and agreement does not encode the size of a score movement. The raw conflict rate remains the substantive non-interchangeability statistic; κ is supplementary.

Machine-readable results: `YAX_V51_KAPPA_AGREEMENT.csv` and `YAX_V51_KAPPA_SUMMARY.json`.
