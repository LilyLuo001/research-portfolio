# Pre-Outcome Rationale for Webb Software Exposure as the Primary Comparison Technology

This is a documentation recovery from the pre-outcome record, not a
post-outcome choice.

`DESIGN_FREEZE_v2.md` fixed Webb software-patent exposure as the primary
computerization control before protected outcomes were opened, with O*NET
computer-use importance as the first robustness control and O*NET level,
Autor-Dorn RTI, and Frey-Osborne automation probability as required
alternatives. The choice was therefore not made because of the realized Webb
coefficient.

The substantive rationale has four parts.

1. **Technology concept.** Webb's common task-patent framework produces
   separate software, robot, and AI exposure measures. The software measure is
   therefore an explicit historical comparison technology rather than a broad
   automation probability or an AI measure relabelled as a control. The AI
   component is never used as the computerization control.
2. **Temporal ordering.** The Webb measure and paper predate ChatGPT and the YAX
   post period. It is consequently a defensible predetermined measure of
   occupational exposure to software-related technological change rather than
   an index updated in response to post-2022 generative-AI diffusion.
3. **Construction.** The measure maps task language to software-patent language.
   Its upper occupational tail is concentrated in process, machine-control, and
   equipment work. That content is distinct from O*NET's rating of how important
   or complex computer interaction is in an occupation.
4. **Design role.** The paper asks what remains of an AI-exposure gradient after
   conditioning on a pre-existing technology margin. A software-specific
   patent-task measure supplies one conceptually explicit answer to that
   question and preserves a task-based construction parallel to the broader
   technology-exposure literature.

Webb is not uniquely correct. Its ceiling ties and unusual occupational ranking
are documented limitations, and its near-zero correlation with O*NET computer
use shows that the two measures do not proxy one interchangeable latent
“computerization” variable. O*NET importance and level capture computer use;
RTI captures routine-task composition; Frey-Osborne captures broad automation
susceptibility. The more-than-twofold coefficient range across these controls
therefore remains a substantive result about conditioning-margin dependence,
not a reason to declare the Webb estimate the true AI effect.
