# YAX Pilot B — Shared Exposure Component Feasibility

**POST-OUTCOME EXPLORATORY — NOT PART OF CONFIRMATORY YAX v1.1**

## Classification: FACTOR-F1

A stable shared statistical dimension survives explicit construction-family balancing
and every leave-one-measure-out diagnostic. It is appropriately called a **shared
exposure component**, never “true AI exposure.”

## Evidence

On 463 occupations with finite values for all six frozen measures and positive
pre-period employment weight, the first principal component of the weighted six-score
correlation matrix explains 80.03% of variance; two components explain 96.11%. The
three AIOE loadings on PC1 are 0.959–0.967, beta and gamma load at 0.945 and 0.951,
and alpha loads much less strongly at 0.466. PC2 is dominated by alpha (loading
0.874). A one-dimensional summary is therefore strong but not exhaustive.

The predeclared family-balanced construction first averages standardized measures
within each family, re-standardizes the AIOE and Eloundou composites, and gives each
family total weight one-half. The two family composites correlate 0.769; each
correlates 0.940 with their equal-family shared component. Leaving out one measure
at a time produces correlations of 0.985–1.000 with the full component. This is not
an artifact of counting three AIOE variants and three Eloundou variants as six
independent votes.

## Architecture-specific structure remains important

Residuals from weighted projection on the shared component are highly aligned with
the AIOE-minus-Eloundou family contrast for the three AIOE measures (correlations
0.945–0.980) and for alpha/beta in the opposite direction (-0.832 and -0.891).
Residuals also retain substantial associations with existing construction-linked
characteristics such as required education, cognitive content, teleworkability, and
wages. The shared component therefore summarizes common rank structure; it does not
make the architectures interchangeable.

Removing an entire family leaves a score correlated 0.940 with the full component,
but one family alone cannot identify a cross-family common dimension. The leave-family-
out rows record this as an identification limitation rather than treating it as a
successful factor replication.

## Gate implication

`FACTOR-F1` supports a narrowly scoped future shared-versus-specific decomposition
proposal. It does not authorize an outcome regression or a wholesale econometric
rewrite. No post-ChatGPT labor outcome entered this pilot.
