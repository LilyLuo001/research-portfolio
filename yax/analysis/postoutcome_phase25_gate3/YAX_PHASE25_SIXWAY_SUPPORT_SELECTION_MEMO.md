# YAX Phase 2.5A — Six-Way Support Selection

**POST-OUTCOME EXPLORATORY — NOT PART OF CONFIRMATORY YAX v1.1**

## Classification: SUPPORT-S2

Selection is visible, bounded, and interpretable. Literal six-way support retains
108,500 of 186,370 harmonized adjacent-month occupational switches (58.22%
unweighted; 58.25% using `LNKFW1MWT`). It is not representative of occupation
families, but it is close to the excluded sample on age, period, and switch-quality
dimensions that bear most directly on the Phase-2C comparison.

## What is and is not selected

The included-minus-excluded standardized differences are 0.062 for age 22–25,
-0.035 for post-2023, 0.030 for young × post, -0.007 for persistent A–B–B moves,
and 0.008 for immediate A–B–A reversals. Origin and destination employment size
also differ moderately rather than dramatically (standardized differences -0.135
and -0.132). The retained sample therefore is not primarily a different age,
period, or transition-quality sample.

Occupation-family composition is selected. Relative to excluded switches, retained
switches overrepresent office and administrative support at origins by 8.34
percentage points and at destinations by 8.77 points; they underrepresent management
by about 7.6 points and building/grounds cleaning and maintenance by about 5.6
points. The largest absolute family-level standardized difference is 0.286. Computer
and mathematical moves are also underrepresented by roughly 4.4 points at origins
and 4.3 points at destinations. Those shifts are economically meaningful and rule
out `SUPPORT-S1`.

## Why transitions are excluded

The three Eloundou measures share the same availability support. Among excluded
switches, 61.94% lack each of alpha, beta, and gamma; 54.93% lack AIOE
ability-direct; 22.05% lack AIOE OEWS/source-weighted; and 15.97% lack AIOE
administrative/equal. Missingness overlaps: 38.85% of excluded switches have exactly
three architectures and 37.11% have five. Thus exclusion mainly reflects construction-
family coverage boundaries rather than random isolated score failures.

## Interpretation bound

The six-way result describes transitions among occupations simultaneously scored by
all six frozen architectures. It must not be generalized to the full transition
universe without the pair-specific audit. The age/time/persistence comparisons make
the retained sample usable; the occupation-family shifts require an explicit support
caveat.

Source: `YAX_PHASE25_SIXWAY_SUPPORT_SELECTION_AUDIT.csv`. No significance test was
used to assign the classification.
