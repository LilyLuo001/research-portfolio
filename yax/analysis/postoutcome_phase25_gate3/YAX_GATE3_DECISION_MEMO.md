# YAX Gate 3 Decision Memo

**POST-OUTCOME EXPLORATORY — NOT PART OF CONFIRMATORY YAX v1.1**

## Gate results

### A. Reallocation validity

- **SUPPORT-S2:** literal six-way support is occupation-family selected, but age,
  timing, persistence, reversal, and employment-size differences are bounded.
- **PAIR-P1:** pair-specific support expands weighted coverage from 58.25% to
  59.54%–90.80%, while all 15 agreement rates move by less than 1.95 percentage
  points relative to six-way support.
- **BENCH-B1:** realized conflict is 53.28% versus a 45.27% matched-marginal mean;
  the 8.02-point excess persists under the A–B–B filter.
- **REALLOC-R1 — STRONG:** the realized-reallocation result survives both support
  expansion and the economically matched reference benchmark.

### B. Dynamic task feasibility

- **ONET-B:** a restricted panel is feasible. Of 923 current-taxonomy occupations,
  267 have multiple genuine pre-2022 task-domain snapshots and a post-2022 snapshot.
  Median positive update spacing is 97 months, and taxonomy, relevance-threshold,
  wording, and source changes prevent an unrestricted panel interpretation.

### C. Shared-component feasibility

- **FACTOR-F1:** a stable shared exposure component survives equal family weighting
  and leave-one-measure-out checks. The two family composites correlate 0.769, PC1
  explains 80.03%, and the minimum leave-one-measure-out correlation with the full
  family-balanced score is 0.985. Architecture-specific residuals remain strongly
  family-linked, so the component is not “true AI exposure” and does not make the
  scores interchangeable.

### D. Architecture-robust inference

Simultaneous architecture-level inference is technically feasible for a declared
vector on common analytic support using the existing occupation-cluster influence
representation and common Rademacher multipliers. It is not valid to treat native-
support estimates with different Q1/Q5 memberships as six noisy estimates of one
parameter. A future max-|t| or joint-region exercise would be a new authorized
inferential battery; none was run at Gate 3.

## Recommended path: PATH-G3-B — CURRENT PAPER + SHARED-COMPONENT EXPLANATION

This is the only recommended path.

`ONET-B` does not clear the high bar for dynamic-task priority. `FACTOR-F1` does clear
the feasibility bar for a narrowly scoped shared-versus-architecture-specific
decomposition proposal, and `REALLOC-R1` leaves the reallocation interpretation usable.
The next step should therefore be a separate pre-analysis proposal that defines the
family-balanced shared component, architecture-specific residuals, common support,
and any joint inference before relating them to outcomes. It must not be a wholesale
econometrics rewrite and is not authorized by this memo alone.

Because `REALLOC-R1` passed, a future destination-cost proposal may use one
predetermined occupation characteristic such as pre-period OEWS wages. It should be
kept separate from the shared-component proposal and was not executed here.

No V5 manuscript was created.
