## What Phase 3 establishes

The transparent family-centroid construction gives equal total weight to the AIOE and Eloundou families. Its shared family component, F, is a statistical common dimension, not true or causal AI exposure. In the only authorized new labor-outcome model, the F-Q5 versus F-Q1 coefficient is -0.12854 log points (occupation-cluster SE 0.04698; one-step wild-score p = .005; 95% CI [-0.21849, -0.03858]), or -12.06% after transformation. The model uses the frozen literal common support of 444 occupations, representing 83.14% of employment. This satisfies SC-A.

Architecture-specific classification remains important for realized occupational movements. Among 108,500 switches, six-way directional conflict declines from 94.59% in the lowest weighted quintile of absolute shared-component movement to 19.06% in the highest, a 75.53-point difference. The persistent-switch difference is 75.93 points. The median architecture-specific displacement summary is 1.40 times as large for conflict as for unanimous switches. This satisfies SC-R1 and supports a descriptive organization: disagreements cluster where moves are small along the shared dimension and comparatively large along architecture-specific dimensions.

That organization does not rescue the stronger benchmark claim. Once rematching preserves age, month, origin broad family, and destination broad family, realized conflict exceeds the benchmark by only 0.96 percentage points. Under the predeclared rule, this is HB-C. Broad assortativity accounts for most of the former 8.02-point excess over independent rematching.

Finally, all six literal-common-support point estimates are negative, but the predeclared simultaneous familywise statement fails. Five simultaneous one-sided upper bounds are below zero; the AIOE administrative-equal upper bound is +0.01858. The intersection-union marginal p-value of .045 does not replace the frozen simultaneous-bound criterion. V5 reports the negative point-estimate pattern and the failed joint statement together.

## Central interpretation

Robustness does not transfer automatically across economic statements. A shared statistical exposure component is sufficient to reproduce the aggregate young-relative employment-stock direction. Architecture-specific dimensions organize whether marginal occupational moves are classified as exposure-increasing or exposure-decreasing. But realized conflict is not meaningfully elevated relative to an assortative rematching benchmark, and the design does not support a familywise 95% claim that all six stock coefficients are simultaneously negative.

All statements are observational. Phase 3 supplies no causal evidence about AI-driven worker movement or employment effects.

## Stopping rule

The selected path is **PATH-P3-C**. Produce the shorter V5 from robust established results, preserve Phase 2 flow nulls in the appendix, and stop. No new outcome, mechanism, exposure measure, timing window, benchmark, heterogeneity analysis, or Phase 4 search is authorized.
