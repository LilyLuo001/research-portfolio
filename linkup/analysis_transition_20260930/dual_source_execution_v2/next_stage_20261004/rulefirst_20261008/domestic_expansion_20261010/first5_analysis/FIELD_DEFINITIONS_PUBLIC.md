# D63 first-five comparison field notes

The analysis uses the frozen rule-first v1.2 evidence and D57 responsibility gate. It does not re-extract or semantically adjudicate advertisements.

- `software_only`, `ai_only`, `software_and_ai`, and `neither_observed` are mutually exclusive partitions of non-negated frozen technology wording among 424,225 processed ads. `neither_observed` is not evidence that technology is absent.
- `any_ai` combines `ai_only` and `software_and_ai` only for the declared primary contrast. The four original groups remain reported.
- Broad experience includes any frozen object association. Narrow experience requires one object, `explicit_rule_candidate`, and `unconditional_explicit_clause`. Related means occupation/task or industry/domain; tool remains separate.
- The output name `independent_judgment_wording` maps to the broader frozen `task_independent_responsibility` family, which includes independent work and ownership wording. It must be described as **independent-work/responsibility wording**, not uniformly as advanced judgment authority.
- D57 mentoring counts only the frozen current-duty candidate gate. Prior mentoring experience and junior referents remain excluded from this duty indicator.
- The explicit eligibility union includes general no-experience wording and graduate wording. Tool/domain-specific waivers are reported separately and excluded from that union. Graduate scope is not stored and is never treated as unconditional.
- `combined_junior_wording_title_or_body_unresolved` cannot separate title from body in the frozen narrow schema.
- Occupation standardization uses official detailed O*NET codes with at least 20 ads in each comparison arm and common weights proportional to the smaller arm count in each occupation. Company-by-occupation sensitivity uses at least five ads per arm. These are predeclared stability thresholds, not accuracy or causal-identification standards.
