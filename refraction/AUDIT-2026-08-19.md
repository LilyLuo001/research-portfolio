# Refraction — execution audit, 2026-08-19

Scope: `refraction/` only. Audited against `docs/Refraction_执行手册_v1_0.md`,
`docs/MacroEvent_Chapter_Plan_v2_1_FINAL.md` (v2.1 final), `ops/contracts/`,
`CLAUDE.md` and `refraction/CLAUDE.md` — not against anyone's recollection of
what was built.

**Headline.** The machinery is in good shape: the two iron rules are program
invariants rather than discipline, all 14 panel assertions exist with a sensible
hard/soft split, 90 tests pass on synthetic fixtures, and nothing has touched a
post-period outcome. The problems are not in the code. They are **four numbers
that Gate-0 needs and does not have**, a **spec conflict between the Plan and the
执行手册**, and a **sample-window mismatch with P1** — all of which must be
settled *before* Gate-0 runs, because settling them afterwards converts a
pre-registered kill-switch into a post-hoc one.

## A. Gate-0 threshold gaps — fix before R3, not during

`执行手册 §R3` requires "判据阈值全部从 frozen_config.yaml 读取" and forbids R3
choosing its own. Four of the six Gate-0 lines could not comply.

| Line | Plan §9 pass line | Was in config? | Action taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | "≥95% of 2017–2026 releases with usable S_a" | **No** | **FIXED** — `surprise_coverage_min: 0.95` added, transcribed from the Plan with a citation. This is transcription, not a choice. |
| **G4** | "*Sufficient* treatment mass with D_b ≥ 0.1" | `d_b_min` yes; the **mass share, no** | **BLOCKED LOUDLY** — `d_b_mass_share_min: null`. There is no number in the Plan to transcribe. |
| **G6** | "All three *flat/zero* over 8 pre-quarters" | **No numeric criterion at all** | **BLOCKED LOUDLY** — `pretrend_joint_p_min: null`. |
| **G2** | `SE(β̂) ≪ SD(L̂)` | `se_to_sdL_ratio_max: 0.3333` | Already present and already flagged as an operationalization of an undefined "≪" — kept, now covered by a test. |

The two nulls are deliberate. Null makes R3 **fail on the config read** instead
of inventing a threshold — iron rule 4 ("don't know → stop") expressed as
configuration. `refraction/tests/test_gate0_config.py` fails if either acquires a
value without a matching owner decision in `ops/decisions.md`.

G6 is the dangerous one. "Flat/zero" with no number is the single most
post-hoc-vulnerable line in the design: whatever the pre-trend plot looks like,
it can be described as flat. DAX's Decision 14 already solved the same problem
(joint p ≥ 0.10 and no Holm-adjusted individual lead) and is the obvious
precedent to adopt.

## B. Plan §9 contradicts 执行手册 §R3 on the G2 joint window — NEED_HUMAN

- **Plan §9** requires a non-empty window of shrinkage weights in which
  **simultaneously**: SD(L̂) ≥ 0.25, |corr(L, ConvExp)| ≤ 0.3, **median pre-period
  announcements ≥ 30**, and SE(β̂) ≪ SD(L̂) for ≥70% of treated names.
- **执行手册 §R3 G2** sweeps only three of those — `[SD≥0.25, corr≤0.3, 占比≥70%]`
  — and treats the ≥30 median as a *separate* G3 line evaluated once, not inside
  the sweep.

This is not cosmetic. Whether the ≥30 median must hold **at the same w_shrink**
as the other three directly determines whether the feasible window is non-empty,
and an empty window is the Plan's own trigger for "stock-level design fails
jointly; portfolio-level becomes primary or kill". Recorded in `frozen_config`
next to `n_pre_median_min`; **the owner must adjudicate before R3 is dispatched.**

## C. Sample window vs the P1 inputs refraction actually consumes

`frozen_config.sample` says `waves_end: 2025-12-31`. The wave file this chapter
consumes (`p1/t2_wrds/waves.csv`, regenerated 2026-08-18) now carries **78 waves
running to 2026-11-20 — 11 of them after that cutoff, and some dated in the
future relative to today.**

Two questions the owner has to answer, neither of which the code can:

1. **Is the 2025-12-31 cap deliberate or stale?** It predates the current P1
   event set.
2. **What is the minimum post-period a wave must have to enter the sample?**
   With `announcements_end: 2026-06-30`, a wave effective 2025-12-31 has roughly
   two post-quarters — against a design built on ±8 quarters, wedge horizons out
   to +60 days, and a reversal-portfolio spine.

**And assertion A2 cannot catch this.** `a2_treated_coverage` checks coverage
*against the calendar*, and the calendar is itself truncated at
`announcements_end` — so a wave with two post-quarters covers "all announcements
in its window" and passes green. The design's post-period requirement is
currently enforced nowhere. A `post_quarters_required` line belongs in
`frozen_config` beside `pre_quarters_required`, and A2 (or a new assert) should
read it.

## D. Methodological caveats to disclose at R4/OSF — not defects

1. **Full-sample standardization.** `S_std = S_raw / (in-sample std)` is exactly
   what C0-R and `frozen_config: surprise.standardize: sample_std` specify, and
   `pipeline/surprises.py` implements it faithfully. But it means an early
   announcement's `S_std` is scaled by variance realized later in the sample.
   This does **not** breach the lookahead ban (rule 4 names β, feature priors, L,
   and basket weights — not the surprise scale), and it is conventional. It is
   still full-sample information entering a pre-period quantity, so it must be
   *stated* in the pre-registration, with expanding-window standardization named
   as the robustness variant rather than discovered as a referee objection.
2. **R13 keyword list lives in `scan.py`, not `frozen_config`.** A deliberate
   deviation from "every tunable lives in frozen_config", documented in
   `scans/manifest.md`: a resident monitor's keywords must stay editable after
   the config freezes without counting as a pre-registration deviation.
3. **The 毛刺 title rule fires on a bare "switch"** and over-flags on purpose —
   a missed priority hit is the expensive error.

## F. What is genuinely good (so the audit is not only a defect list)

- Both project iron rules are **machine-enforced**: `prereg_guard.assert_prereg_ok()`
  refuses to run while the OSF timestamp is absent or `w_shrink` is null, and
  `a4_no_lookahead` / `assert_no_lookahead` enforce the lookahead ban as code.
  A test confirms both are still unfrozen.
- **14/14 panel assertions** present, with A5 correctly *soft* (it records the
  n_pre distribution for Gate-0 G3 rather than passing judgement itself).
- **No post-period outcome column has been touched** by anything in this
  directory, and the R6+ path stays guard-blocked by design.
- The pieces built ahead of their blockers were the **schema-free** halves —
  R1b's transform, the R1a fetch mechanics — with `parse_usmpd()` raising
  `NeedInfo` rather than guessing USMPD's columns.

## The four things to settle before Gate-0

1. **G4 mass share** — fill `d_b_mass_share_min`.
2. **G6 flatness criterion** — fill `pretrend_joint_p_min` (DAX Decision 14 is the precedent).

specification search no matter who does the deciding, which is why none of them
has been filled in here.
