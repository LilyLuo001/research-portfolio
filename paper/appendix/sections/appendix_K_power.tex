\section{Prospective precision and code audit}\label{app:power}

The pre-outcome headline simulation implied a standard error of 0.01217; the realized analytic standard error was 0.04441, a ratio of approximately 3.65. For the paired beta-minus-alpha contrast, the prospective standard error was 0.01167 and the realized paired standard error was 0.03697, a ratio of approximately 3.17. The frozen paired design therefore had 80 percent power to detect differences of approximately 0.0327 log points, but the prospective precision calculations are design-history provenance and are not evidence of realized power.

The code-only audit shows that the simulation used the actual exposure distribution, employment stocks, design matrix, weights, residual paths, and grouped estimator. It did not independently allocate equal information to all nominal occupations. Treatment concentration and leverage therefore entered mechanically, although no effective-information target was calibrated separately. Comparing $\sqrt{468/43.3}\approx3.29$ with the precision ratio is only a scale heuristic: the 43.3 value is fitted estimator information, while the prospective residual-treatment effective count was 53.263. The audit does not support a quantitative decomposition. The classification is that the code does not support excessive nominal-information diffusion as the explanation; the available evidence does not isolate a unique cause.
