\section{Prospective precision, realized resolution, and failed diagnostics}\label{app:power}

The prospective primary SE was .01217 and the realized occupation-cluster SE is .04441, a ratio of 3.649. For beta minus alpha, .01167 versus .03759 gives a ratio of approximately 3.22 on the updated paired fit. The frozen paired design had 80-percent power to detect coefficient differences of approximately 3.27 percentage points under its planning DGP. No verified published estimate matched the YAX age groups, stock estimand, Q5--Q1 contrast, and scale, so binding equivalence inference was retired rather than assigned an arbitrary smallest effect of interest.

At realized precision, the primary normal-theory MDE$_{80}$ is .1244 log points. Architecture-pair MDEs range from .0609 to .1689. Those values belong beside the architecture comparisons because they explain why point-estimate variation and statistical distinguishability are different claims.

The original historical simulation preserved observed exposure, weights, design matrices, and ordered residual paths. It omitted survey sampling, new postperiod shocks, structural composition change, exposure measurement uncertainty, and several dependence structures. A new sharp sensitivity preserves an entire donor month's contemporaneous cross-occupation residual vector by applying one global sign. All 66 cyclic donor offsets and both signs yield 132 paths. The mean analytic SE is .012168, essentially identical to .012170 under the independent-sign simulation, while the empirical SD is .006591. The sensitivity closes none of the gap to .044410. It rules out this global-sign implementation as the explanation; it neither validates the original power calculation nor establishes that other common shocks are irrelevant.

Two failed diagnostics remain part of the record. The occupation-specific age-season model did not converge with 5,148 additional nuisance parameters. A literal residual-wild full-refit attempt produced no admissible grouped-binomial pseudo-outcome in 199 draws because transformed successes left $[0,N]$. That particular pseudo-outcome construction is inappropriate; its failure neither invalidates nor validates the one-step score bootstrap. No clipping or outcome-favorable substitute was introduced.
