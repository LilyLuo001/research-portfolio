\section{Prospective precision and failed diagnostics}\label{app:power}

The prospective primary SE was .01217 and realized SE .04441, a ratio of 3.649. For beta minus alpha, .01167 versus .03697 gives 3.167. Estimands, support, units, and inference targets match. The ex-ante paired design had 80-percent power to detect differences of approximately 3.27 percentage points, but no verified literature-comparable smallest effect of interest existed; binding equivalence inference was retired rather than assigned an arbitrary threshold.

The historical simulation preserved observed exposure, weights, design matrices, and ordered residual paths. It did not preserve contemporaneous cross-occupation covariance and did not include survey noise, new post shocks, structural composition change, exposure measurement error, or misspecification. Available variants do not identify their separate contributions. Comparing $\sqrt{468/43.3}$ to the precision ratio is only a scale heuristic because 43.3 is fitted information, not independent clusters or the simulated information target.

Two failed diagnostics remain part of the record. The occupation-specific age-season model did not converge with 5,148 additional nuisance parameters. The requested literal residual-wild full refit yielded zero admissible pseudo-outcomes in 199 attempts for both the primary and no-alpha F/G models because successes left grouped-binomial bounds. No clipping, alternative bootstrap, or favorable substitute was introduced.
