\section{Calendar, mapping, and sample flow}\label{app:mapping}

The original extract selected March ASEC (`03s`) rather than March basic (`03b`) for 2017--2021. ASEC records carry zero basic monthly final weights and cannot fill the intended cells. A revision extract restores the five basic samples. October 2025 remains absent because no CPS was collected during the federal government shutdown. Combined raw files contain 9,843,021 rows and 114 observed survey months; the corrected static model contains 113 after omitting December 2022.

Pre-2020 records use an official Census-2010-to-2018 route bridge. Conversion proportions are common to young and older observations within source occupation and month, mechanically preserving each source ratio across its components before aggregation. Fifty-six source occupations are one-to-many, maximum multiplicity is seven, and these sources represent 20.03 percent of early weighted employment.
