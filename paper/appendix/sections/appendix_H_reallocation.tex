Zero-threshold conflict is 35.02 percent for the five-measure no-alpha set and 11.35 percent within AIOE; movement-mass-weighted shares are 15.35 and 2.10 percent. At .25 SD, substantial opposition is 12.77 percent without alpha and .33 percent within AIOE. These comparisons change the number of potential pairs and are not marginal effects of deleting a measure.

Immediate reversals (6,187 switches) and persistent A--B--B changes (39,893) have zero-threshold conflicts of 55.15 and 54.46 percent. At .5 SD, substantial-opposition shares are 13.57 and 14.39 percent. Transition-boundary exclusions are applied symmetrically; no September-to-November 2025 false adjacent links and no MISH4-to-5 long-gap pairs remain.

Across architectures, weighted shares moving higher range from 49.21 to 50.49 percent; destination top-rank-quintile shares range from 19.96 to 23.39 percent. The switch-only frame cannot classify entrants from nonemployment.

The main benchmark uses 30,170 strata and preserves detailed origin/destination margins within age-by-month and broad origin/destination cells. A no-self repair eliminates all generated identical detailed origins and destinations while preserving margins. The exact unconstrained product-marginal conflict is 50.2893 percent across 32,075 strata but permits self-transitions with probability 3.6591 percent; it is not the exact expectation for the constrained null. The 0.96-point excess is a sample finding close to the predeclared one-point threshold, not an equivalence conclusion.

Named threshold-surviving examples include accountants to bookkeeping clerks; physician assistants to diagnostic technologists; registered nurses to medical and health-services managers; and retail salespersons to cashiers. Complete score movements and weights are stored in the machine-readable results.
