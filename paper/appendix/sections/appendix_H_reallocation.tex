\section{Mobility disagreement and an algorithm-defined benchmark}\label{app:reallocation}

The mobility exercises are post-outcome exploratory diagnostics of constructed
classifications.  They use 108,500 employed-to-employed switches on six-way
common support and official longitudinal weights.  They neither estimate a
hiring margin nor validate a latent occupation code.

\subsection{Identical support, omitted-support bounds, and uncertainty}

Hamilton allocation at 200,000 pseudo-units represents 84,192 of 94,893
detailed transition cells, 97,759 of 108,500 switch rows, and 98.305 percent of
official weight.  Recomputing both sides on that identical represented support
gives the following audit.

If conflict on the omitted 1.695 percent of benchmark support is unrestricted
in $[0,1]$, the all-support benchmark lies in [51.4359,53.1307] percent and the
all-support realized-minus-benchmark gap lies in [0.1521,1.8469] percentage
points.  The sign survives this accounting bound, but the economic magnitude is
not pinned near one point.

The sealed rule begins with a uniform destination permutation within each
age-by-month-by-broad-origin-by-broad-destination stratum, repairs the first
self-match using a feasible random swap, and restarts after an impasse.  It
preserves detailed margins and removes self-transitions, but it is not uniform
over feasible derangements.  An alternative that chooses the bad self-match
uniformly before choosing a feasible partner gives 52.3211 percent, versus
52.3227 percent under the sealed rule; the difference is $-0.00157$ percentage
points.  This is one repair-rule sensitivity, not proof of a unique random-
matching distribution.  The defensible comparison is therefore ``relative to
the specified broad-assortative no-self benchmark,'' not a causal explanation
by broad assortativity.

\subsection{Reversals do not identify a coding-error matrix}

The earlier 9.865-percent reversal statistic used 11,121 A--B--A sequences
among 112,736 observable first switches in a broader ages-18--65 feasibility
universe.  In the present ages-22--65 six-way support, 6,187 reversals are 10.336
percent of 59,858 switches with an observable third interview (10.663 percent
under official weights), or 5.948 percent of official weight over all 108,500
switches.  An A--B--A sequence may reflect a real temporary move, an assignment
change, proxy reporting, or coding error.  It does not reveal latent true
occupation or a five-category error matrix.  No attenuation curve is therefore
constructed: under unknown differential error, Q5--Q1 misclassification may
attenuate, amplify, or reassign the contrast.
