\section{Continuous family models and exact reparameterization}\label{app:fgae}

Let $A$ and $E$ be equal-weight centroids of the three standardized ability- and task-family implementations. Raw $F=(A+E)/2$ and $G=(A-E)/2$ are separately standardized in the fitted model with Webb. The original construction has weighted SDs .8777 and .3221 and weighted correlation .2177; neither raw construction nor standardization makes the fitted coordinates orthogonal.

A representative two-score model estimates administrative AIOE .0449 (interval [.0012,.0885]) and beta $-.0788$ (interval $[-.1292,-.0284]$). The direct primitive model estimates $D=-.0323$ (interval $[-.0617,-.0030]$) and $S=\gamma-\alpha=-.0303$ (interval $[-.0562,-.0044]$), conditional on Webb.

For separately scaled regressors, the exact basis transformation is
\[
\gamma_A=\tfrac12(b_F/s_F+b_G/s_G),\qquad
\gamma_E=\tfrac12(b_F/s_F-b_G/s_G).
\]
Consistently transforming every draw gives $\gamma_A=.0248$ (paired SE .0199) and $\gamma_E=-.0707$ (paired SE .0272). This is the same fitted predictor, not corroboration. A smaller variance alone does not mechanically create a larger standardized coefficient; covariance with the residualized outcome and with other regressors also matters.

For $D+\lambda S$, the categorical Q5--Q1 estimate is $-.1013$ at $\lambda=0$, $-.1256$ at $.25$, $-.1297$ at $.5$, $-.1432$ at $.75$, and $-.1482$ at $1$. Continuous slopes differ under fixed beta scaling and restandardization, so both conventions are stored. The combined evidence supports construction-sensitive asymmetry, not a universal family ordering.
