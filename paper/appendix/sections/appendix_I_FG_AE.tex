\section{Consensus, family disagreement, and exact change of basis}\label{app:fgae}

Let $A_o$ be the equally weighted centroid of the three standardized ability-based architectures and $E_o$ the corresponding task-based centroid. Define
\begin{equation}
F_o=\frac{A_o+E_o}{2},\qquad G_o=\frac{A_o-E_o}{2}.
\end{equation}
$F$ is a family-balanced consensus coordinate and $G$ is a family-disagreement coordinate. Both are statistical constructions, not latent causal factors.

The frozen joint model enters standardized $F$, standardized $G$, and Webb jointly as continuous young-by-post slopes on 444 occupations and 108 months. With 999 common occupation multipliers, $\widehat b_F=-0.04036$ with interval $[-0.06925,-0.01147]$, and $\widehat b_G=0.03089$ with interval $[0.00180,0.05999]$. Their centered covariance is $-0.00009825$; the joint max-$|t|$ $p$-value is 0.008.

The A/E presentation is an exact algebraic change of basis of this same regression. Per weighted standard deviation, the AIOE centroid coefficient is 0.02493 and the Eloundou centroid coefficient is $-0.06148$. It is not a second model. The A-minus-E contrast inherits the stored wild-score inference for $G$ exactly; individual A and E intervals use the serialized covariance matrix and the normal critical value because draw-level F/G shifts were not retained.

Treatment-side reconstruction shows that the family-disagreement coordinate is materially affected by alpha. Occupation deletion leaves the fitted $G$ coefficient's sign stable, so the conditional result is not a single-occupation artifact; however, the meaning of the family coordinate remains sensitive to how the task-based family is constructed.
