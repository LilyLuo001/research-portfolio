\section{Longitudinal flow estimands and results}\label{app:flows}

All flow models use valid exact adjacent-month CPSIDV links, origin age, official origin LNKFW1MWT, January 2017--November 2022 as pre, January 2023 onward as post, and December 2022 as transition. Links across the MISH 4-to-5 rotation gap are excluded. December 2019 origins are excluded from occupation-dependent transitions.

For exit, the risk set is employed origins and the event is nonemployment at $t+1$. For occupational outflow, the risk set is employed origins linked to an employed destination and the event is a different harmonized occupation. For entry destination, the risk set is nonemployed origins linked to employment; the outcome is allocation toward destination Q5 rather than Q1. Consequently, the three coefficients are not additive decompositions of the stock coefficient.
