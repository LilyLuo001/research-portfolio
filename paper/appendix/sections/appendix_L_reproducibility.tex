Public-use CPS microdata are obtained from IPUMS and are not redistributed. Principal private-file hashes, not paths or credentials, appear in execution receipts. Scripts write aggregate results only. Every major workstream has a self-check or machine audit covering result identities, support counts, input/output hashes, and the absence of raw person identifiers.

The scientific article no longer uses ``referee-requested,'' ``R1-style,'' or ``R2-style'' to motivate an exercise. Revision chronology belongs here and in the response letter. The reason for each main-text analysis is stated in economic terms.

Author affiliation, email, acknowledgments, funding, conflicts, and final journal data-use language remain for author completion; none has been fabricated.
