\section{Reproducibility provenance}\label{app:reproducibility}

The design freeze peels to commit \nolinkurl{22fbf7924809b7a535e31ae0ab68f5b113ce8078}; the confirmatory-results tag peels to \nolinkurl{b16109482c3bf5ca176f6f08976e120b04769945}. Frozen receipts record input hashes, code versions, support membership, multiplier seeds, and output hashes. Confirmatory analyses comprise the signed alpha/beta measurement architectures, stock models, paired-difference inference, and mapping and coverage rules. Common-support six-measure comparisons, estimator-support diagnostics, refined event-time analyses, flows, reallocation, and the F/G joint model are labeled exploratory.

The public-use CPS microdata are obtained through IPUMS CPS under its terms of use and are not redistributed in the repository. Exposure inputs and official crosswalks are either vendored where licenses permit or referenced by provider, version, and checksum. Scripts generate analysis files from documented relative paths; credentials, usernames, hostnames, and absolute machine paths are excluded.

This production pass changes prose, layout, tables, and figures only. It estimates no new empirical specification, changes no treatment definition, and creates no new bootstrap multipliers. Numerical claims are inherited from sealed outputs or exact algebraic transformations of them.
