\section{Estimator and inference audits}\label{app:inference}

For two age groups, conditioning Poisson means $(\mu_y,\mu_o)$ on their weighted total yields the grouped-binomial pseudo-log-likelihood $N_y\log p+N_o\log(1-p)$ with $\operatorname{logit}(p)=\log(\mu_y/\mu_o)$. Occupation-month effects cancel. CPS final weights build $N_y$ and $N_o$ and are not applied again to the objective. These continuous weighted totals define an estimating equation; they are not literal independent binomial trials. A multinomial age-profile extension requires its own derivation.
