\section{Inference and dependence}\label{app:inference}

The stock models use occupation-clustered analytic standard errors and one-step wild-score confidence intervals with stored, common occupation multipliers where paired or joint statements require covariance preservation. The common-support comparison uses 999 common multipliers. Separate point-estimate signs, the predeclared simultaneous one-sided band, and the intersection--union test are distinct statements.

All six common-support point estimates are negative. The simultaneous one-sided critical value is 2.26036; the administrative-AIOE upper bound is 0.01858, so the predeclared all-six simultaneous-band criterion is not met. The separately auditable intersection--union marginal $p$-value is 0.045 and does not supersede the declared criterion.

The paired beta-minus-alpha contrast is $-0.03240$, with paired standard error 0.03697, 95 percent interval $[-0.10235,0.03755]$, and $p=0.403$. The interval supports only the statement that the design does not detect a difference; it does not establish economic equivalence.

An exploratory occupation-by-calendar-month inclusion--exclusion covariance leaves the main inferential pattern unchanged. It is a model-based dependence sensitivity, not a reconstruction of the CPS survey design, because the public analysis extract lacks the strata, PSU, and replicate-weight structure required for that exercise.
