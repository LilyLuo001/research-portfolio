\section{Estimator, precision, and time-dependence audits}\label{app:inference}

\subsection{Estimating equation and maintained randomness}

For two age groups, conditioning Poisson means $(\mu_y,\mu_o)$ on their weighted total yields the grouped-binomial pseudo-log-likelihood $N_y\log p+N_o\log(1-p)$ with $\operatorname{logit}(p)=\log(\mu_y/\mu_o)$. Occupation-month effects cancel. CPS final weights construct $N_y$ and $N_o$ and are not applied again. These continuous stocks define an estimating equation rather than literal independent binomial trials.

The primary sandwich and wild-score interval treat occupation score contributions as independent clusters and allow arbitrary serial dependence within each occupation. They condition on the observed cells, treatment labels, mapping, support, weights, and model. They do not propagate the CPS complex-survey design, exposure-label generation, or taxonomy-allocation uncertainty. In the nuisance-adjusted implementation, regressors are absorbed against occupation and calendar-month effects under fitted binomial information weights. Scores are summed within occupation and premultiplied by the inverse information matrix. A $\sqrt{G/(G-1)}$ finite-cluster correction is applied. Each bootstrap draw multiplies the whole occupation influence vector by one Rademacher sign; the analytic cluster SE is the studentizer. Paired analyses reuse the same occupation signs.
