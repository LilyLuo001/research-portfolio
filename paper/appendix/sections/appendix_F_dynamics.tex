\section{Dynamics and population-control diagnostics}\label{app:dynamics}

\subsection{Event-time specification}

The event-study model replaces the single Q5-by-young-by-post coefficient with month-specific Q5-by-young interactions, retaining occupation-by-age, occupation-by-month, age-by-month effects, Q2--Q4 post terms, and the Webb software interaction. October 2022 is the coefficient reference month. December 2022 remains in the event-study data as the declared product-launch transition month but is not assigned to the static post indicator; the static model omits it. October 2025 is absent because no CPS was collected. The distinction between a reference coefficient and a transition-period rule resolves the earlier caption ambiguity.

\subsection{January 2025 population controls}

BLS introduced Vintage-2024 population controls in January 2025, raising the estimated civilian noninstitutional population by approximately 2.9 million and employment by approximately 2 million. Official prior months were not revised. BLS's experimental bridge is aggregate and supplies no age-by-occupation adjustment, so a literal subgroup counterfactual weight series is unavailable.
