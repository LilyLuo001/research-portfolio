\section{Dynamics, pretrends, and comparison technologies}\label{app:dynamics}

The categorical pretrend joint test does not detect a differential Q5-versus-Q1 pretrend in the pre-period. The post path is non-immediate and non-monotone. These diagnostics guard against a simple linear differential trend, but absence of a detected pretrend does not identify generative AI as the cause of a post-2022 break. Plausible contemporaneous changes include the technology-sector correction, interest rates, return-to-office policy, post-pandemic normalization, and occupational-composition shifts.

Remote-work analyses are reported as competing-interpretation sensitivities. They do not absorb the beta gradient, but neither occupation-level feasibility nor realized telework among employed respondents can rule out remote-work mechanisms. In particular, own telework hours are observed conditional on employment and are not used as an individual-level explanation of employment status.
