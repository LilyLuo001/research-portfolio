Within-SOC2 permutation holds each broad family's beta score multiset fixed and reassigns scores across detailed occupations. Among 999 successful fits, the coefficient mean is $-0.1097$ (SD .0268); the observed $-0.1311$ has lower-tail probability .219 and centered two-sided probability .434. Exchangeability is not established, so this is an assumption-dependent diagnostic.
