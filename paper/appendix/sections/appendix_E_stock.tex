\section{Employment-stock estimates}\label{app:stock}

The stock outcome is occupation--age-group--month employment, estimated in a conditional Poisson model with saturated occupation--age, occupation--month, and age--month fixed effects. The target is the post-period change in the young-versus-older employment ratio in exposure Q5 relative to Q1, conditional on the frozen comparison-technology interaction. It is neither an individual unemployment probability nor a monotone dose-response parameter.
