\section{Fixed-treatment occupation influence}\label{app:loco}

The leave-one-occupation-out audit deletes each occupation while retaining frozen treatment assignments and refits the same model. It is an influence diagnostic, not a redefinition of treatment. The primary full-sample estimate is $-0.131074$; deletion estimates range from $-0.14238$ to $-0.11055$, with no sign reversal. The largest absolute movement is 0.02052.

Fast food and counter workers are in frozen Q1; deleting them moves the estimate to $-0.110553$ (movement $+0.020521$). Customer service representatives are in Q5; deleting them moves the estimate to $-0.113112$ (movement $+0.017962$). Software developers are in Q5 and rank tenth by absolute influence; deleting them moves the estimate to $-0.138787$ (movement $-0.007713$). Thus, the result is not a software-developer artifact, while influential contributions arise at both exposure tails.
