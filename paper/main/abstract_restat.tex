Recent evidence associates generative-AI exposure with weaker employment among workers in their early twenties. I reconstruct this in public monthly CPS data. The estimand is the post-December-2022 change in the log conditional-mean employment-stock ratio of ages 22--25 to 26--65 in the highest relative to lowest GPT-4-exposure quintile, conditional on intermediate quintiles and preperiod software exposure. Its estimate is $-0.132$ log points; broad occupation-family paths reduce it to $-0.022$, while direct tail support spans 29 occupations. Preperiod dynamics reject parallel evolution. The pattern is a descriptive comparison across broad occupational structure, not an identified causal AI effect.
