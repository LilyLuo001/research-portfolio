Using public monthly CPS data, I estimate the post-December-2022 change in the log conditional-mean employment-stock ratio of ages 22--25 to 26--65 in the highest versus lowest GPT-4-exposure quintile. The pooled estimate is $-0.132$ log points, but only 29 occupations---5.03 percent of preperiod stock---directly support within-family tail comparisons. Broad occupation-family paths reduce the estimate to $-0.022$. Annual ACS data improve person-sampling precision without repairing occupational support, and preperiod dynamics reject parallel evolution. The association therefore relies predominantly on cross-family comparisons and does not identify a causal AI effect.
