Occupational AI exposure is constructed before it enters a regression. Holding a CPS employment-stock design fixed, I vary technology scope, labels, aggregation, taxonomy, support, and representation. Taxonomy repair raises computer-and-mathematical employment coverage from 3.33 to 97.7 percent through re-admission. Six implementations from two selected families yield negative common-support tail estimates, but Webb AI produces a smaller, imprecise contrast and an OECD capability measure produces nearly zero. The original $-0.1311$ beta contrast is reference-dependent. Mobility disagreement is common but falls with movement size. Robustness belongs to a specified construction and economic statement, not the label ``AI exposure.''
