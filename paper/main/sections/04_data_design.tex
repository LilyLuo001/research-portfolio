\section{Estimands, Design, and Inference}\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Headline objective}

Let $N_{oat}$ denote the CPS-weighted employment stock in occupation $o$, age group $a$, and month $t$. Let $Y_a$ identify ages 22--25, $P_t$ identify January 2023 onward, $Q_{oq}$ denote exposure quintile $q$, and $W_o$ denote standardized Webb software exposure. The conditional mean is
\begin{equation}
E[N_{oat}|X]=\exp\left[\gamma_{oa}+\delta_{ot}+\lambda_{at}+\sum_{q=2}^{5}\beta_q Q_{oq}Y_aP_t+\theta W_oY_aP_t\right].\label{eq:headline}
\end{equation}
The fixed effects are occupation-by-age, occupation-by-month, and age-by-month. The target $\beta_5$ is the post-period change in the young-to-older employment-stock ratio for Q5 relative to Q1, conditional on Q2--Q4 and Webb. It is neither a continuous exposure slope nor an individual probability effect.

With two age groups, conditioning independent Poisson pseudo-likelihood contributions for $N_{oyt}$ and $N_{oot}$ on $T_{ot}=N_{oyt}+N_{oot}$ yields a grouped-binomial contribution
\begin{equation}
 \ell_{ot}=N_{oyt}\log p_{ot}+N_{oot}\log(1-p_{ot}),\qquad
 \operatorname{logit}(p_{ot})=\log\frac{\mu_{oyt}}{\mu_{oot}}.\label{eq:conditional}
\end{equation}
Occupation-month terms cancel from the ratio; the remaining fixed effects and interactions reproduce the implemented objective. CPS person weights construct the two cell totals. Those totals are estimating-equation inputs, not literal counts of independent survey-weighted binomial trials, and they receive no second likelihood weight. The six-bin age profile is multinomial and is not justified by this binary derivation.

The primary sample has 50,544 potential occupation-month cells, of which 13,305 have zero young stock, 1,036 zero older stock, and 965 both zero. Estimation retains 49,579 nonempty cells. Minimum full-panel respondent-equivalent thresholds of 100, 250, and 500 produce coefficients between $-0.1311$ and $-0.1326$. A separately constructed unweighted respondent-equivalent outcome gives $-0.1308$; it changes the cell estimand and does not double-weight CPS stocks.

\subsection{Inference and information}

The frozen inference clusters by occupation and uses 999 common occupation-level Rademacher wild-score multipliers. Shared multipliers preserve covariance for paired architecture and reference-group contrasts. A two-way occupation/calendar-month model-based sensitivity changes the primary SE only from 0.0444 to 0.0449; neither is a complete CPS survey-design correction.

Four information objects must remain separate. The primary model has 468 nominal occupation clusters. Webb-residualized beta variation has inverse-Herfindahl effective support 53.26 and a top-five share of 22.15 percent. Fitted conditional information has effective count 43.30 and top-five share 24.57 percent. Actual leave-one-occupation-out movement is at most 0.0205 and never flips the coefficient sign. Effective counts are concentration summaries, not numbers of independent clusters.

The all-six sign statement illustrates compound inference. For
\[
H_0:\text{at least one }\beta_m\geq0,\qquad H_1:\beta_m<0\text{ for every }m,
\]
the marginal one-sided tail areas are .045, .005, .006, .008, .001, and .001, so the intersection--union $p$-value is their maximum, .045. The predeclared simultaneous one-sided band is a different deliverable: its critical value is 2.26036 and the limiting administrative-AIOE upper bound is +0.01858. That criterion fails and remains visible.

Realized uncertainty also exceeded planning calculations. Primary and paired realized/prospective SE ratios are 3.649 and 3.167 on matched estimands, support, and units. The planning simulations omitted contemporaneous cross-occupation covariance, survey noise, new post shocks, structural change, measurement error, and misspecification. Existing artifacts cannot identify a unique allocation of the gap. A literal residual-wild full refit was attempted for the primary and no-alpha F/G models, but all 199 pseudo-outcomes violated grouped-binomial bounds; no clipping or different bootstrap was substituted.

Confirmatory choices were frozen before protected outcomes were opened. Common-support, age, calendar repair, placebo, external-architecture, family-refit, and mobility audits are post-outcome exploratory. This chronology limits claims but does not conceal important failures.
