\section{Continuous and Family Reparameterizations}\label{sec:family}

The categorical results consume changing tail memberships. A continuous exercise asks a different question: conditional on Webb software exposure, how do the two selected families divide residual occupational variation? Let $A$ be the equal-weight centroid of three standardized AIOE implementations and $E$ the analogous Eloundou centroid. Define raw coordinates $F=(A+E)/2$ and $G=(A-E)/2$, then standardize $F$ and $G$ separately in the implemented model. $F$ is family-balanced consensus and $G$ family disagreement; neither is a latent true exposure.

The original exploratory model estimates $b_F=-0.0404$ with interval $[-0.0674,-0.0133]$ and $b_G=+0.0309$ with interval $[0.0029,0.0589]$. The reported original exploratory $G$ tail area was .040; the identical refit under a new seed gives .032, reconciling rather than replacing the earlier number.

The requested leave-one-measure outcome refits change the interpretation. Removing alpha leaves $F=-0.0330$ and $G=+0.0342$, with both intervals excluding zero. Thus the original asymmetry is not alpha-only. Removing any AIOE implementation also leaves the $G$ interval above zero. But removing beta yields $G=+0.0287$ with interval $[-0.0009,0.0584]$, and removing broad yields $+0.0215$ with interval $[-0.0080,0.0511]$. The disagreement result is therefore materially sensitive to which task-family implementations receive equal family weight.

A representative one-AIOE model is more transparent: entered jointly, administrative AIOE is $+0.0449$ (interval $[0.0012,0.0885]$) and beta is $-0.0788$ (interval $[-0.1292,-0.0284]$). A direct primitive model estimates $D=-0.0323$ and $S=-0.0303$, with both intervals below zero. These are alternative parameterizations and conditioning exercises, not independent corroborations.

Because the fitted regressors are $F/s_F$ and $G/s_G$, the exact original-unit family transformation is
\begin{equation}
\gamma_A=\tfrac12\left(b_F/s_F+b_G/s_G\right),\qquad
\gamma_E=\tfrac12\left(b_F/s_F-b_G/s_G\right).\label{eq:ae}
\end{equation}
Transforming every common draw consistently gives $\gamma_A=+0.0248$ (paired SE 0.0199) and $\gamma_E=-0.0707$ (paired SE 0.0272). This is an exact change of basis of the same fitted predictor, not a second outcome model. The raw $F$ and $G$ weighted correlation is 0.218 in the original construction and varies under deletion; standardization does not imply orthogonality.

The $D+\lambda S$ grid reaches the same caution from within the task family. All five categorical Q5--Q1 estimates are negative, from $-0.1013$ at $\lambda=0$ to $-0.1482$ at $\lambda=1$, while fixed-normalization and restandardized continuous coefficients differ. The family evidence is best summarized as a negative consensus association plus construction-sensitive conditional asymmetry. It does not rank exposure families universally.
