\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

An occupational ``AI exposure'' variable is already an empirical model before it reaches a regression. Its authors have chosen a technology boundary, an occupational primitive, a source of labels, and an aggregation rule. A downstream researcher then chooses a taxonomy bridge, support rule, normalization, conditioning set, and treatment representation. These choices can change both the score and the economic comparison it defines.

This paper asks which of those choices alter which conclusions. Robustness is statement-specific. A high-versus-low coefficient consumes tail membership and a reference group. A continuous regression consumes residual variation after controls. An occupational-mobility claim consumes pairwise ranks and a threshold for an economically meaningful move. Agreement for one object does not imply agreement for the others.

I use early-career employment as a demanding application. The fixed design compares the post-January-2023 evolution of CPS employment stocks for ages 22--25 and 26--65 across occupational exposure groups. It was originally motivated by the age gradient in private payroll data documented by \citet{brynjolfsson2026canaries}. The CPS analysis does not observe AI adoption, and its occupation-level timing is not an instrument. Its role here is to stress-test a constructed treatment while holding the outcome and estimator fixed.

The architecture audit begins with two selected families and six implementations: three ability-based AIOE variants \citep{felten2018method,felten2021occupational}, and direct, software-complemented, and broad task-based LLM measures \citep{eloundou2024gpts}. These are not six independent observations of one known latent exposure. I then admit two outside architectures under an outcome-independent rule: Webb's AI patent--task measure and the OECD capability-gap measure \citep{webb2020impact,oecd2026exposure}.

Five results determine the revised framing. First, taxonomy repair raises computer-and-mathematical employment coverage from 3.33 to 97.7 percent. On fixed support it barely changes a continuous AIOE coefficient, from $-0.01885$ to $-0.01920$; most of the change to $-0.03156$ comes from readmitting occupations. Second, the six selected implementations all give negative common-support tail point estimates, but this does not extend to the wider architecture set: Webb AI gives $-0.0649$ with an interval spanning zero, and the OECD measure gives $-0.0110$.

Third, the original beta tail estimate of $-0.1311$ log points is reference-dependent. Q5 differs from Q1 and Q3, but its differences from Q2 and Q4 are much smaller and not detected. Separate Q1 and Q5 stock paths and deletion checks show that favorable low-tail evolution contributes to the contrast. Fourth, the beta estimate is more negative than education, cognitive-content, and telework benchmarks on identical support, but a within-broad-occupation permutation diagnostic does not place it unusually far in the lower tail. AI specificity is therefore not established.

Fifth, occupational ranking disagreement is real but economically scale-dependent. At a zero threshold, 53.28 percent of linked occupational switches receive opposite directions from at least two of the six measures. Weighting conflict by movement mass lowers the share to 40.10 percent; requiring substantial opposition of at least 0.5 standard deviations lowers it to 14.00 percent. Yet each architecture classifies roughly half of aggregate switch weight as moving upward. Architecture choice is important for marginal rankings without overturning the aggregate direction of reallocation.

The contribution is a linked measurement audit. It follows construction through taxonomy coverage, support, tail membership, estimator information, employment contrasts, and mobility statements. This complements sensitivity work that asks which moments drive estimates \citep{andrews2017sensitivity} and work on uncertainty in trend-based designs \citep{rambachan2023credible}, without borrowing identification that the CPS design does not possess. It also preserves the paper's design record: a predeclared all-six simultaneous-band criterion failed, and realized standard errors were more than three times prospective calculations.
