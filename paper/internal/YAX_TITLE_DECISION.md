# Title decision

Decision: **retain** the current title.

> What Is AI Exposure? Measurement Architecture and Statement-Specific Robustness in Early-Career Employment

Alternatives considered:

1. *Robust to What? Occupational AI Exposure and Early-Career Employment* — concise, but it hides the paper's main contribution: architectures construct different treatment objects.
2. *Constructing AI Exposure: Rankings, Support, and Early-Career Employment* — accurate, but it underplays the general statement-specific robustness principle.
3. *Six Measures of AI Exposure and Early-Career Employment* — transparent but too application-specific and risks sounding like a conventional robustness comparison.

The retained title names the measurement question, the conceptual contribution, and the empirical laboratory without implying causality, observed adoption, or a universal ranking of exposure families.
