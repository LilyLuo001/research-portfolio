# Finite scientific decision rules — no new empirical claim

This document applies the approved method prompt, not a new research plan. No research outcomes were opened to formulate these rules. The candidate is a **measurement and interpretation** contribution; it does not identify causal concentration effects or the origin of privately produced information.

## Keep the estimand distinct from the story

The measured object is the ETF-versus-actual-basket difference in response to an externally defined company news signal, scaled by a verified common terminal response when that scaling is identified. It is not Hasbrouck information share, percentage of companies efficiently priced, or a proportion of all announcement information. EPS-conditioned responses do not encompass guidance, revenue or every other simultaneous message.

Stratum-standardized slope ratios compare a specified common-support population. They are not means of company-level speed ratios. News-conditioned linear projections remain signal-variance weighted within a stratum unless further model assumptions hold. Equal issuer weights alone cannot establish a population-average structural response. No method implementation may silently remove these limits.
