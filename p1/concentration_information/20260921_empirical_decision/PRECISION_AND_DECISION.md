# Scientific decision — development evidence, not causal certification

## Decision boundary

Do not promote the proposed concentration/ETF price-discovery story to a supported causal result or a powered confirmatory design. Daily association can be measured with the existing SCC inputs; that is different from establishing information transmission, ETF mediation, or a change in market ecology caused by concentration.

The current development execution has 11 announcement dates and 10 event-window overlap blocks. Its reused control-window support links those dates into two components (sizes 10 and 1). These are graph diagnostics, not effective sample sizes. Event-block bootstrap intervals ignore some shared-control dependence and cannot certify precision or power. Component collapse limits this proposed resampling procedure; it does not refute every possible design.

There is no pre-approved economically meaningful effect threshold for this estimand. No empirical power or minimum detectable effect is certified. A large stock-event row count does not replace independent shocks, and a small or negative point estimate is not an equivalence test proving no spillovers.

## What the pilot measures

The daily primary unsigned metric is the weighted mean of individual absolute two-session stock returns, contrasted with each stock's preselected non-event controls. It is not the absolute return of the aggregate basket. Focal announcing companies are removed, so their own index arithmetic is not counted as receiver movement. This exclusion alone does not remove common fundamentals, macro news, related earnings, selection, or ETF coholding endogeneity.

The network exposure is baseline reported-weight coholding with the actual announcing issuer. It is not contemporaneous ETF trading, flows, or ownership dollars. A connection slope is an association and cannot identify an ETF transmission mechanism. Same-industry and size/liquidity controls narrow some comparisons but do not establish exclusion restrictions.

Date-only earnings metadata cannot rule out an own-company release after the previous trading close. Named macro flags cover CPI, Employment Situation and FOMC only. Neither the main nor macro-excluded sample is certified free of competing news.

## Minute branch

The bounded quote diagnosis is complete, but scientific minute-response estimation remains unavailable. Short initialization histories and missing status information do not prove that a stock had no live quote. The July Exxon publisher-clock conflict remains unresolved. No new purchase was made; acquiring more quotes without a settled clock would not fix the measurement problem.

## Validation integrity

The H1 daily-return query excludes H2 responses. Earlier executions nevertheless loaded full-year delisting-return fields in process before joining H1 rows. The independent date-only recount found 452 H2 rows among 890 annual delisting records. Those H2 values were not used in the H1 estimates or returned to the model, but the literal claim that all H2 sources were never read is false. The final corrected query and finite review must record this incident. Any future held-out study needs an explicit exposure ledger; it must not quietly call the entire H2 source stack pristine.

## Contribution boundary

An earnings/coholding association alone is not a new contribution: [Bhojraj, Mohanram and Zhang's ETF information-transfer paper](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/Bhojraj_Mohanram_Zhang.pdf) already studies cross-firm earnings responses associated with ETFs. This one-period baseline network also cannot show a historical change caused by rising concentration. Such claims require separate evidence rather than a more attractive interpretation of the same regression.

## Corrected numerical synthesis

The executed H1 sample contains 15 issuer-date events, 5,025 broad receiver-date observations and 6,658 observed-positive issuer-receiver observations. The corrected industry mapping covers all eight announcing issuers; the same-industry subset has 514 observations and 142 receivers. Independent recomputation matches the corrected representative network coefficients to numerical precision.

| Diagnostic | Point estimate | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Equal-weight mean individual absolute event response minus controls | -0.173 percentage points | No positive broad unsigned-activity pattern in this development sample. |
| Fixed pre-period market-cap-weight counterpart | -0.239 percentage points | Broad conclusion does not reverse with this weighting. |
| Equal-weight absolute matched-basket contrast, secondary | -0.418 percentage points | Same receiver mask and weights in each event/control comparison; no interval certified. |
| Fixed-weight absolute matched-basket counterpart, secondary | -0.359 percentage points | Same qualitative direction for whole-basket movement. |
| Full-network adjusted unsigned contrast per one SD of log coholding | -0.183 percentage points | Does not support the hypothesized general amplification. |
| Same-industry adjusted unsigned contrast, same exposure scale | +0.260 percentage points | A narrower exploratory pattern; not ETF mediation or a validated cross-group difference. |

The same-industry unadjusted coefficient is +0.243 percentage points. Adding size and liquidity controls does **not** shrink its point estimate; its heuristic interval becomes less decisive, crossing zero. None of these intervals is fully adjusted for the observed shared-control structure. No significance or power conclusion follows. Separate subgroup slopes also do not by themselves test a between-group interaction.

The whole-basket diagnostic was implemented after the pilot as a disclosed secondary supplement, without changing the primary mean-individual-absolute metric. It contains 48 matched event/control comparisons over 40 distinct control windows, with 393–489 identical receiver constituents within each comparison. It is not an independent confirmatory test.

## One recommended next action

**Continue only with one prospectively frozen, daily-data replication of the narrow same-industry coholding pattern, using disjoint event/control calendar support in a separately logged, not-yet-estimated date sample. Do not buy more quotes for the broad story now.**

Before opening that sample, specify the direct same-industry versus other-industry interaction and its counterfactual, calendar dependence, economic threshold and exposure ledger. This is a new scientific specification decision, not an automatic unsealing authorized by these exploratory numbers. Use existing SCC daily inputs first. If independent calendar support cannot be obtained, stop this proposed replication rather than count shared controls as new information.

The recommendation is therefore **HOLD_DESIGN for the strong concentration/ETF causal claim; a bounded descriptive replication is worth considering, not research GO**. Minute price-discovery measurement separately remains **HOLD_DATA**. The original MF-to-ETF conversion identification is not revived by this stage.
