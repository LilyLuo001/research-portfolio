## Finite review delta

1. **Issuer-industry construction, repaired.** The initial full script selected the 2022-12-30 issuer SIC after dropping observations without a prior-row market cap. Because December 30 was the only 2022 return date loaded, every cutoff observation was removed. Independent inspection found all 6,658 issuer SIC values missing, zero same-SIC rows, and a mechanically deficient adjusted design. Those failures cannot be interpreted economically. The finite network-only repair uses date-valid pre-response cutoff identities and retains missing SIC as unknown. Independent reinspection verified zero missing issuer SIC, 514 same-SIC rows / 142 receivers, and full-rank adjusted designs (four broad-sample columns, three same-industry columns).
2. **Delisting source seal, exposure-boundary failure.** The initial script projected full-year `dlret` without an H1 source predicate, although only H1 company returns entered its joins/results. A separate date-only projection independently verified 890 annual source rows, including 452 dated after June 30; no `dlret` was projected in that check. Those H2 delisting rows were therefore loaded into the original execution process; it is inaccurate to say all H2 outcome-bearing sources were untouched. Correcting the predicate prevents future exposure but does not erase that history. No H2 values were requested or displayed during this review. The receipt must distinguish source access from analytic use.
3. **Precision limitation, not a new specification search.** Event-overlap blocks do not account for all reused control windows. The reported bootstrap is heuristic, not a valid confirmatory interval or empirical-power certificate. Calendar-support connected components diagnose this procedure's dependence; they are not an effective sample size and do not disprove every alternative design.
4. **Outcome and exclusion labels, corrected/qualified.** The daily unsigned output is now explicitly labeled `mean_stock_absolute_response`, not the absolute aggregate-basket return. A distinct after-pilot basket supplement has been produced and verified below. Date-only own-news filters cannot eliminate releases after the preceding trading day's close. Neither broad exclusions nor three macro-release families establish a news-clean counterfactual. Source-date coverage is not a certification of complete valid-return contributions. The final README and decision document retain these limits.

## Scientific interpretation constraint

A negative development-sample unsigned contrast does not disprove every information-transmission channel. It does, however, fail to establish the proposed positive amplification story in this implementation. Even a positive association would not by itself establish ETF mediation, an effect of increasing concentration, novelty, or causal identification. The final decision appropriately retains the positive same-industry pattern as exploratory, recognizes that subgroup slopes are not a tested interaction, and does not treat shared-control intervals as valid power evidence. Its one proposed next action—a prospectively specified independent-calendar-support daily replication, with a new exposure ledger and no automatic unsealing—is consistent with the evidence. More quotes cannot resolve the present identification and dependence limitations.
