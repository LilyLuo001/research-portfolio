## Question

Replicate the exploratory same-industry issuer/receiver coholding association in a not-previously-estimated date sample. Test both the same-industry slope and its direct interaction difference from other-industry receivers. Do not compare subgroup significance labels. The outcome is absolute two-session receiver return minus its mean absolute matched-control return; this is not information speed, accuracy, or an ETF causal effect.

Statistical significance does not supply an unapproved economic materiality threshold. An absent economic threshold does not block metadata or descriptive work but prohibits an economic-pass claim.

## Terminal decisions

- `STOP_SUPPORT`: result-blind support is insufficient for the proposed replication. Preserve actual artifacts; no new outcomes read. At most one precise additional source/date request.
- `CONTINUE_MECHANISM_FEASIBILITY`: the narrow association and direct difference have interpretable replication support; not causal research GO.
- `STOP_SIGNAL_ROUTE`: sufficiently discriminating evidence does not support further investment in this specified signal.
- `INCONCLUSIVE_STOP_SPENDING`: evidence cannot discriminate adequately; not a zero-effect conclusion and no automatic expansion.
