# Phase 2 decision

Decision date: 2026-09-23.

## `CONTINUE_PAPER — INCOMPLETE_ACTIVITY_SUBSTITUTION / COMMON_PRICE_RESILIENCE`

The 2023-01-24 NYSE opening-auction failure produced the empirical separation Paper 1 needs. In the saved 0-through-300-second window, three-feed NYSE-listed trade notional was only 35.8% of its normal median and alternative cash venues did not expand above their own normal activity enough to replace the missing auction. Yet SPY and ESH3 remained inside their normal opening consistency band. The three raw cash-basket exceedances were each caused by one crossed-composite constituent being dropped, not a broad cross-sectional dislocation; the independent review therefore rates the result `LIMITED_PASS` rather than an unrestricted pass.

This warrants continuing the paper around the distinction between **transaction-capacity redundancy** and **common-information resilience**. It does not warrant saying that ETFs caused resilience, that ETF trading replaced all missing cash activity, or that firm-specific price discovery was preserved.

The current case is a paper-level empirical result, not merely an engineering pilot. The manuscript should center the verified activity-versus-price-resilience separation and disclose the XNYS degraded condition, 301-bucket window and basket-composite limitation. Any additional event is external-validity evidence, not a search for a preferred sign.
