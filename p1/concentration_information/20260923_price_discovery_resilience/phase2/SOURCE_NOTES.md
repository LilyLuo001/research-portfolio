# Source notes: bounded 2019–2025 event screen

The screen is a bounded source review, not a market-wide incident census. The primary regulatory source for the retained case is the SEC's settled order, Release 34-104934 (6 March 2026), pages 1 and 5–8: it identifies the systems chain, 2,824 NYSE-listed securities, the 09:30 failure to run opening auctions, 84 LULD pauses, about 10:09 internal discovery, the 10:21 member update, and busted trades. Source: <https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/admin/2026/34-104934.pdf>.

NYSE's current auction documentation confirms the normal mechanism: NYSE-listed securities use the NYSE opening auction, while NYSE Arca and NYSE American have distinct auction processes. It is a mechanism reference, not an incident timestamp source. <https://www.nyse.com/trade/auctions>

The 2020 rows were deliberately kept separate. They are market-wide circuit breakers during COVID-era volatility, not exchange technical failures and not LULD incidents masquerading as exogenous venue outages. The NYSE filing records the March 9, 12, and 16 events and the March 9/12 clocks; an SEC-hosted IEX filing records all four March 9/12/16/18 events and their 15-minute halt rule: <https://www.sec.gov/file/34-88413>; <https://www.sec.gov/rules/sro/iex/2020/34-90128.pdf>.

Search coverage: NYSE/SEC produced the retained contemporary candidate and the market-wide circuit-breaker exclusion records. Nasdaq's primary market-system-status archive was searched as an operator source (<https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/trader.aspx?id=marketsystemstatussearch>), but within this one-pass bounded screen did not yield a second event with an auditable affected-security interval and an economically comparable cash/ETF/ES alternative. Cboe and CME operator materials were searched for a comparable U.S.-equities channel loss but did not produce such a candidate in this pass. CTA/SIP status and the exact NYSE symbol-level LULD/reopen/correction sequence remain required source gaps for the retained case. No response, return, spread, volume, or information-share outcome was inspected to retain or exclude any row.
