## What is genuinely good

Meta-rule 1 held throughout — every number carries an EDGAR accession, a WRDS
query locator or a URL, and `[NEED_PDF]`/`NEED_HUMAN` markers are used honestly
rather than guess-filled. The power simulation ran *before* the sample was final.
The kill-switch gate was cleared on committed numbers. The free EDGAR path was
built against a parallel contract without renaming a frozen column.

And the DFA concentration was discoverable at all only because the treatment
variable was built, committed and inspectable — which is what the pipeline
discipline was for. It surfaced before any outcome existed, which is the one
timing that makes it a design decision rather than a specification search.
