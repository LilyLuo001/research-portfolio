## Part 2 — is it done properly, per recent literature?

Three checks run today. One is a real gap.

### 🔴 L-1. Continuous-dose DiD needs an assumption the plan never names

ConvExp is a **continuous** treatment intensity, and §8 item 4 makes
"强度连续 vs 二元 vs 三分位" a robustness axis. The current reference on this is
Callaway, Goodman-Bacon & Sant'Anna, *Difference-in-Differences with a Continuous
Treatment* ([NBER w32117](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32117),
[arXiv 2107.02637](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.02637)).

Their result matters directly here: under the **standard** parallel-trends
assumption, comparisons of treatment effects *across dose levels* are
contaminated by selection bias arising from treatment-effect heterogeneity.
Identifying a dose-response (ACRT / derivative) parameter requires a
**strong parallel trends** assumption — that the average change from untreated to
dose *d* is the same for all units as for those who actually received dose *d*.
Even when strong parallel trends holds, TWFE weights on causal responses differ
from the dose distribution among the treated, which complicates interpreting the
TWFE coefficient.

P1's plan §T5 does flag that CS and Sun–Abraham need "适配方案" under continuous
intensity and asks for the assumption differences to be stated — so the *question*
is on the board. What is missing is the specific answer: **strong parallel trends
must be stated as an explicit identifying assumption, defended, and tested where
possible**, or the continuous-dose results must be reported as ATT-by-dose-group
rather than as a dose-response curve.

This is a spec-stage fix. It belongs in the T5 blueprint, which has not been
written — so it costs nothing today and would be expensive to retrofit after
estimation.

### 🔴 L-2. `econlib` cannot estimate the design as specified

Confirmed by inspection:

```
callaway_santanna(df, y, unit, time, first_treat)
stacked_did(df, y, unit, time, first_treat, window)
twfe_did(df, y, unit, time, treat)
```

All three are **binary / staggered-adoption** estimators keyed on `first_treat`.
A grep for `dose|intensity|continuous|conv_exp` across `shared/econlib/` returns
nothing. There is no continuous-treatment estimator in the portfolio's shared
toolkit, and P1's main treatment variable is continuous.

So T5 as written cannot be implemented today even with data in hand. Either
econlib gains a continuous-dose estimator (seat D owns `shared/`), or T5 commits
to binary/tercile treatment as primary with the continuous version demoted.
**That is a design decision, and it should be made before WRDS arrives, not
during the sprint.**

### 🟡 L-3. Event-set completeness against the industry count

Industry reporting puts conversions at **203 over five years, 60 in 2025 alone**,
with the 200 mark crossed in May 2026
([VettaFi / Advisor Perspectives](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/commentaries/2026/05/29/cross-200-mutual-fund-etf-conversions),
[ETFdb](https://etfdb.com/fixed-income-content-hub/mutual-fund-etf-conversions-cross-200/)).

P1 has **131 events**, of which **36 are equity_US**:

| asset_class | n |
|---|---:|
| equity_US | 36 |
| fixed_income | 36 |
| equity_intl | 25 |
| other | 9 |

P1's 2025 count is 36 events (all classes) against an industry 60 for that year.
The gap may be entirely definitional — P1's universe, the N-14/N-PORT filing
basis, and what trade press counts as a "conversion" need not agree. **But it has
never been reconciled**, and the event list is the foundation of everything
downstream. A one-page reconciliation against a published count is cheap
insurance against a referee doing it first.

Also: `AUM_at_conversion_USD` is **NA for all 36 equity_US events**. Dose comes
from N-PORT holdings so estimation does not need it, but no descriptive table of
conversion size can be produced, and §8's DFA-dominance discussion is much harder
to write without it.
