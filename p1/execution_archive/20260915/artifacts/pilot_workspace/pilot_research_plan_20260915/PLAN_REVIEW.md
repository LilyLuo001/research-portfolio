## Scientific assessment

1. **Identification and power are correctly separated.** The primary object is a differential package effect on signed SUE response slopes among selected positive-ownership stocks. Low exposure is treated, conversion timing is endogenous, and neither a favorable pretrend test nor a precise curve establishes the counterfactual. The plan names sponsor shocks, selection, spillovers and surprise stability as assumptions and limits timing/AP claims. More quotes cannot repair these assumptions. Its current HOLD_DESIGN + HOLD_DATA is supported; NO_GO for the entire research topic would be premature.

2. **Acquisition selection is not silently made scientific eligibility.** Terciles precede analyst/quote screening, and the 71-name purchase roster and full-12-event analyst screen are explicitly distinguished from the target population. The first attrition table must preserve that distinction: report full population, competing-conversion exclusions, event-level analyst eligibility, balanced-12 screen, acquisition cap and session/common-mask losses separately. The reported high-tier scarcity is a serious warning, but the balanced-screen intersection alone does not establish the final feasible wave set. Outcome-blind coverage selection can still be endogenous; a mask table documents rather than cures that limitation.

3. **The exact estimator is testable and can legitimately fail.** Equal event-row fitting, fixed stock weights, wave aggregation, controls and the observed stock-by-calendar-cell requirement are retained. The latter is unusually demanding for sparse earnings panels; generic matrix rank cannot substitute for the required observed Cartesian support. The explicit NOT_ESTIMABLE outcome and single target amendment are appropriate. No control deletion, unsupported prediction or stress-wave promotion should be used to rescue the original estimand.

4. **Dependence is treated as a design constraint.** Unique economic events, overlapping return-leg dates, stocks and sponsors matter; 852 associations are not 852 independent treatment shocks. Graph collapse appropriately stops that multiplier procedure. Few sponsors may leave no defensible confirmatory inference even with many earnings events. A single alternative memo must state what provides repeated independent information and why its assumptions fit this design. Simulated size under an assumed dependence model is not evidence that those assumptions hold in reality.

5. **PRE-only calibration is a coherent pilot boundary.** Preserving joint horizon vectors, event reuse, nuisance uncertainty and transport stress is preferable to a sample-size formula. POST eligibility can determine a frozen layout without revealing POST response values. However, generated POST SUE only provides conditional/generic rank and conditional precision. Realized full-design rank and surprise stability remain unverified until an authorized later stage; the decision language must preserve that distinction.

6. **The work can end.** The plan supplies bounded work packages, partial outputs, one source pass, a capped simulation extension, a limited inference alternative and one minimal data request. Immediate P0/P1/P3 work can identify structural impossibility without a renewed general Gate 1 pass. Measurement repairs block the affected measurements, not every branch. No additional broad audit is needed to accept this plan.
