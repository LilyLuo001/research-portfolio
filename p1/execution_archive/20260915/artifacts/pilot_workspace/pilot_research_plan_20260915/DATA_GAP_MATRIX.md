## Decisive design limitations

1. The data can support a measurement-and-design pilot, but not yet a causal earnings-slope DID: treatment timing, SUE, endpoint masks, controls, and dependence are unfinished.
2. The proposed clean design currently loses two main waves entirely and leaves the stress wave thin; relaxing overlap rules or promoting W016 changes the target rather than repairing power.
3. The binding precision unit is the package/sponsor/economic-event dependence structure, not 852 association rows or millions of one-second records.
