## Causal assumptions

At fixed comparable surprise and predetermined covariates, conversion-absent **changes in earnings-response slopes** must be parallel across the specified groups. Average-return parallel trends do not suffice. Conversion may change coverage, forecasts and measured SUE; require invariant measurement/selection or explicit standardization to a common overlapping population. Otherwise the slope is a conditional association involving a treatment-affected regressor.

Within-fund comparisons remove additive shared shocks but not exposure-proportional fund shocks. Existing ETF saturation, Russell overlap, peer information spillovers, other conversions, and changing earnings composition remain threats. For a package effect, post-conversion flows, fees, clientele and holdings can be mediators: the plan's controls and continuity-based exclusions can change the target. They cannot establish an AP-only effect. Strong cross-dose transport is required to interpret a high-minus-low difference as a dose increment on the same population; tercile binning does not remove selection. Pretrend non-rejection is not proof.
