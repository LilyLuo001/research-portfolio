## Adjudication

The setting and generic earnings-efficiency outcome are not novel. The defensible conditional increment is narrower: whether a predeclared conversion-package exposure contrast shifts the **within-event horizon shape** of the underlying stock's signed earnings response while leaving an economically specified terminal response approximately unchanged. That increment remains `HOLD_DESIGN` because the amplitude-proof contrast, comparator, target population and equivalence margin are not signed; it remains `HOLD_DATA` because release timestamps and live quote inputs are absent. Zero search hits would not prove novelty.
