## W002: listing, fee change and announcement are different evidence

Dimensional's June 14, 2021 issuer notice identifies DFUS, DFAC, DFAS and DFAT as four converted US tax-managed funds listed that day. Its footnote separately places an associated management-fee decrease at June 11. This supports common issuer/constituent and concurrent-fee-change evidence, not a pure wrapper-only shock. It does not establish the earliest announcement or choose between legal completion and first trading for the contract's implementation clock. [Issuer notice](https://www.dimensional.com/us-en/newsroom/dimensional-lists-four-new-etfs-following-the-industrys-largest-mutual-fund-to-etf-conversion).

The November 16 filing has a prospectus marked subject to completion November 17 and registers six successor ETFs. Targeted searches for predecessor references did not establish the mutual-fund-to-successor conversion announcement. Neither date should be silently promoted to a verified four-predecessor announcement. [SEC 485APOS, 000179420220000482](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1816125/000179420220000482/form485a.htm). The separately checked November 17 issuer essay explains ETF structure, not the required predecessor conversion link. [Issuer essay](https://www.dimensional.com/us-en/insights/dimensional-investing-in-an-active-etf-structure).

## W032: the November date was a proposal, not evidence of completion

The August 20, 2024 N-14 names the Large Cap Equity and Small-Mid Cap Equity fund/ETF pairs. It presents November 15 reorganization and November 18 trading as expected, bracketed dates, with postponement possible. Thus the historical November value cannot certify implementation. [Original N-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/875186/000119312524203605/d869411dn14.htm).

A subsequent SEC prospectus describes both reorganizations as occurring December 9, 2024. This gives later completion evidence, distinct from the original proposal. [Later 485BPOS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/875186/000095013025000314/d767768d485bpos.htm).

Issuer fact-sheet search excerpts instead describe asset acquisition at close of business December 6 and use December 9 as the predecessor-history cutoff. Those snippets are additional date-type evidence, not a fully inspected primary timing record. Preserve the distinction pending source-specific extraction; do not overwrite either field with a generic effective date. [Large-cap fact sheet](https://www.morganstanley.com/wealth-investmentsolutions/msps/mslc.pdf), [small/mid-cap fact sheet](https://www.morganstanley.com/wealth-investmentsolutions/msps/mssm.pdf).
