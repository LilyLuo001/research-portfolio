## What V3 verifies

- Pooled portfolio, share class, ETF security, underlying position/security,
  economic date, and availability timestamp are separate index domains.
- Monthly TNA is a share-class quantity; same-date portfolio TNA is a derived
  sum over economically active mapped classes.
- Holdings `report_dt` is the economic date and `eff_dt` is a date-level
  availability field. No intraday holdings timestamp or verified monthly-TNA
  publication timestamp is available in the audited inputs.
- A holdings row is a pooled-portfolio position line. `market_val` is in U.S.
  dollars, and its raw-record market-value reconciliation passed.
- `percent_tna` is in percentage points and uses same-date pooled portfolio TNA
  as its denominator. The frozen weight formula is `percent_tna / 100`.
- Missing exact-date class TNA is not carried forward, carried backward, or
  nearest-matched.
- ETF-class dollar exposure is constructed only for date-scoped, verified
  pro-rata claims, using portfolio weight times same-date ETF-class TNA.
- Historical ETF-status/flag anomalies are treated as negative controls;
  `et_flag` alone is not accepted as an ETF conversion date.
