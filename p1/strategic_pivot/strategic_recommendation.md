## Decision

**Recommended primary project: “What changes when an existing investment strategy acquires ETF architecture?”**

Lead with fund-level MF→ETF conversions and monthly fund outcomes. Use historical Vanguard and modern multi-sponsor ETF-share-class activations as exact-same-portfolio validation and mechanism evidence. Keep high-dose stock transmission as a secondary result. Do not run the original broad stock-level headline, and do not run Refraction/FOMC treatment regressions unless their pre-specified first stages pass.

## Why this is the strongest path

The original stock design has 8,801 positive stock-wave cells, but the median predecessor ownership dose is only 0.0299%, the high-dose observations occur in four waves, and sponsor/wave concentration dominates. Conditional power grids cannot be finalized without outcome variance, and no TAQ outcome archive exists. More rows do not repair a small treatment dose or create independent shocks.

At the fund level, wrapper treatment is complete rather than fractional: 156 completed conversions are identified, 74 have exact verified dates, and 71 events across 47 waves already pass N-PORT Gate0. CRSP Mutual Funds and N-PORT can support monthly flows, assets, fees, distributions, turnover, concentration, and portfolio implementation without TAQ.

Share-class activations improve identification but not current raw power. Vanguard has 70 shared ETF/mutual portfolios, yet only 19 satisfy the frozen clean-stagger rule and those fall on nine dates. The modern census has 10 confirmed new ETF-class activations through the cutoff, one reverse-direction event that must remain separate, and ten announced Dimensional events that are still pending. These are excellent validation and future expansion samples, not a present substitute for 47 conversion waves.

## One-paper hierarchy

1. **Primary fact:** full conversion changes fund demand/flows and portfolio implementation relative to matched nonconverters.
2. **Identification validation:** same-portfolio ETF-class activations reproduce the predicted direction without eliminating the mutual wrapper.
3. **Mechanism:** ETF shares outstanding/flow or creation-redemption activity moves after architecture activation.
4. **Secondary transmission:** unusually high pre-event holdings identify which underlying stocks are most exposed.

Do not pool full conversions and share-class additions into one coefficient. They are complementary interventions with different estimands.

## Direct answers to the strategic questions

- **Continue the original broad stock ATE?** No. Preserve its infrastructure, but stop it as a headline.
- **Why did it fail?** Both treatment dose and independent event count. The median dose is economically tiny, and the upper tail is concentrated in four waves; raw stock count solves neither problem.
- **Usable historical Vanguard activations?** 19 clean staggered activations on nine distinct dates, out of a 70-portfolio shared-class census.
- **Modern launches today?** 10 verified new ETF-class activations by 2026-09-03. Keep the one reverse-direction event and ten pending events separate.
- **Are ETF share classes cleaner than conversions?** Yes for isolating access to ETF trading/AP machinery because the legal portfolio continues unchanged. They are not currently better powered.
- **Is a fund-level conversion paper identifiable?** Credibly possible, not yet proven. It must pass matched overlap, pretrend, anticipation, sponsor-selection, and continuity gates.
- **Is the high-dose stock design credible?** Only as a few-wave mechanism exercise, never as the general headline.
- **Did Refraction pass its first-stage kill test?** No pass/fail result exists. The test cannot be completed from the current data because creation/redemption/basket data are not acquired; absence of evidence is not a pass.
- **Two papers or one?** Do not preserve two full papers now. Consolidate the strongest conversion and share-class components into one disciplined fund-architecture flagship. Archive Refraction/FOMC as a conditional extension unless its first stage later passes with an independently adequate macro sample.
- **Highest realistic JF/JFE/RFS probability?** The fund-level conversion paper, strengthened by same-portfolio share-class validation. Its probability is conditional on the frozen design gates; no path is yet top-journal-ready.
