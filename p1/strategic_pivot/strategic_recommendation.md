## Superseded 2026-09-03 decision (retained for audit)

**Recommended primary project: “What changes when an existing investment strategy acquires ETF architecture?”**

Lead with fund-level MF→ETF conversions and monthly fund outcomes. Treat historical
Vanguard and modern multi-sponsor ETF-share-class records only as candidate
same-series/CRSP-portfolio validation observations until prospectus objective,
first-trade, pro-rata, and continuity checks are complete. Keep high-dose stock
transmission as a secondary result. Do not run the original broad stock-level
headline, and do not run Refraction/FOMC treatment regressions unless their
pre-specified first stages pass.

### Why this was considered the strongest path

The original stock design has 8,801 positive stock-wave cells, but the median predecessor ownership dose is only 0.0299%, the high-dose observations occur in four waves, and sponsor/wave concentration dominates. Conditional power grids cannot be finalized without outcome variance, and no TAQ outcome archive exists. More rows do not repair a small treatment dose or create independent shocks.

At the fund level, wrapper treatment is complete rather than fractional: 156
completed conversions are identified and 74 have exact verified dates. A legacy
artifact recorded 71 events across 47 waves with PRE/POST N-PORT filing coverage.
That historical count is unaudited under V3 and its former Gate0/PASS label is
invalid; it is not authoritative for eligibility or portfolio continuity.
CRSP Mutual Funds and N-PORT may support monthly flows, assets, fees,
distributions, turnover, concentration, and portfolio implementation without
TAQ, subject to the post-V3 decision and a new feasibility pilot.

Share-class activations could improve identification if their institutional
relationship is verified, but not current raw power. Among 70 Vanguard
same-series/CRSP-portfolio records, 19 mechanically satisfy the historical
stagger rule on nine dates; none is yet clean for research use without
prospectus-objective, first-trade, pro-rata, and realized-continuity checks. The
modern census has 10 confirmed new ETF-class activations through the cutoff, one
reverse-direction event that must remain separate, and ten announced Dimensional
events that are still pending. These are candidate validation and
future-expansion samples, not a substitute for a conversion sample whose usable
pre-treatment-eligible count is currently unknown.

### One-paper hierarchy

1. **Primary fact:** full conversion changes fund demand/flows and portfolio implementation relative to matched nonconverters.
2. **Identification validation:** only institutionally verified same-portfolio,
   pro-rata ETF-class activations test the predicted direction without eliminating
   the mutual wrapper.
3. **Mechanism:** validated ETF shares outstanding/flow or creation-redemption
   activity moves after architecture activation.
4. **Secondary transmission:** unusually high pre-event holdings identify which underlying stocks are most exposed.

Do not pool full conversions and share-class additions into one coefficient. They are complementary interventions with different estimands.

### Direct answers to the strategic questions

- **Continue the original broad stock ATE?** No. Preserve its infrastructure, but stop it as a headline.
- **Why did it fail?** Both treatment dose and independent event count. The median dose is economically tiny, and the upper tail is concentrated in four waves; raw stock count solves neither problem.
- **Usable historical Vanguard activations?** Not established. There are 19
  mechanical same-series/CRSP-portfolio candidates on nine dates in a 70-record
  census, pending prospectus-objective, first-trade, pro-rata, and continuity
  verification.
- **Modern launches today?** 10 verified new ETF-class activations by 2026-09-03. Keep the one reverse-direction event and ten pending events separate.
- **Are ETF share classes cleaner than conversions?** Potentially, once the same
  pooled portfolio and pro-rata relationship are verified historically. They are
  not currently validated or better powered.
- **Is a fund-level conversion paper identifiable?** Credibly possible, not yet
  proven. It must pass pre-treatment identity, matched overlap, pretrend,
  anticipation, and sponsor-selection checks; realized continuity is reported
  diagnostically and cannot select the headline sample.
- **Is the high-dose stock design credible?** Only as a few-wave mechanism exercise, never as the general headline.
- **Did Refraction pass its first-stage kill test?** No pass/fail result exists. The test cannot be completed from the current data because creation/redemption/basket data are not acquired; absence of evidence is not a pass.
- **Two papers or one?** Do not preserve two full papers now. Consolidate the strongest conversion and share-class components into one disciplined fund-architecture flagship. Archive Refraction/FOMC as a conditional extension unless its first stage later passes with an independently adequate macro sample.
- **Highest realistic JF/JFE/RFS probability?** The fund-level conversion paper,
  potentially strengthened by institutionally verified same-portfolio
  share-class validation. Its probability is conditional on the scientific
  gates; no path is yet top-journal-ready.
