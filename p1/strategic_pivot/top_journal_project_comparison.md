## What would change the ranking

Candidate 2 can be reconsidered only if the novelty and F0 gates establish the
capital bridge, pre-treatment eligibility, donor support, and pretrends.
Realized holdings continuity remains descriptive and cannot select the sample.
Candidate 3 additionally requires institutional verification of the historical
same-pool/pro-rata relationship and event clocks. Candidate 4 needs those checks,
at least one year of post-launch data, and wider sponsor adoption. Candidate 6
requires an observed creation/redemption first stage and enough independent FOMC
dates.

The ranking deliberately does not reward the number of stock rows. It rewards
independent shocks, observable treatment intensity, pre-treatment identity, and
a defendable assignment mechanism.

## Ranking of the four proposed headline questions

1. **Candidate 2 — asset-management behavior after the same strategy acquires an ETF channel.** Strongest conditional candidate. It aligns the fully treated fund-level unit with observable monthly outcomes and could use independently verified same-portfolio observations.
2. **Candidate 3 — an exchange-traded claim on an unchanged portfolio.** It could
   be the cleanest experiment if the historical relationship is verified, but
   currently has too few independent candidate dates to carry the paper alone.
3. **Candidate 1 — ETF architecture and information transmission into asset prices.** Economically important, but the current P1 stock dose and Refraction first-stage/data deficits make the claim premature.
4. **Candidate 4 — unified omnibus.** Reject in its listed form. A disciplined architecture paper may combine conversions and share classes, but earnings, FOMC, TAQ, every fund outcome, and every market-quality outcome cannot all be coequal headlines.
