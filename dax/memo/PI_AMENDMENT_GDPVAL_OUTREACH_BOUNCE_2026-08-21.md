## Exact amended PI decision

> Because the 2026-08-21 GDPval outreach email bounced and no author contact
> was established, the original 14-calendar-day waiting rule no longer serves
> its intended purpose and is superseded. I authorize immediate activation of
> the already-approved qualified-human task-duration fallback. This amendment
> is made before any human-duration annotation result exists. Run the
> prospectively frozen stratified 40-task pilot first, using at least three
> independent real qualified human annotators per task. Do not substitute LLM
> estimates, synthetic durations, aggregate means, occupation averages, or
> semantic-neighbor assignments. All previously signed qualification,
> independence, blinding, lower/median/upper active-minute elicitation,
> adjacent-bin agreement, privacy, no-imputation, stopping, and later-valid-
> author-data-precedence rules remain unchanged. The pilot threshold remains
> adjacent-bin agreement of at least 80%, with no systematic task-family
> failure. This amendment does not authorize unlimited human-annotation
> spending and does not modify Mapping A, W4, W5, identification, power, or
> outcome gates.

## Consequences

1. The fallback is immediately applicable; the project must not wait until
   `2026-09-04T13:04:55Z` merely because the superseded rule named that date.
2. Sample selection, qualification rules, and elicitation materials must be
   frozen before any pilot response is collected.
3. Real-human recruitment, consent/confidentiality, payment, and any necessary
   IRB determination remain operational prerequisites. This amendment is not
   an unlimited budget authorization.
4. If valid exact-version task-level author data later become available from a
   verified source, they take precedence for matching tasks under the original
   source-validation rules. Human work is not delayed while waiting for them.
5. The duration gate remains blocked until all 220 tasks have valid durations.
